Why we link instead of republish

Local newsrooms are paid by attention. An aggregator that lifts a paragraph and keeps the reader on its own page takes that attention and gives nothing back, and then the newsroom shrinks and there is less reporting for everyone — including for us, because most of what a records request confirms was first noticed by a reporter.

So the rule here is absolute: link out, never republish. The only documents hosted on this site are public records, which belong to the public already.
