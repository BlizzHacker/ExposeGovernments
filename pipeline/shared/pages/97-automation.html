This chapter refreshes itself on a weekly timer: it pulls new agendas and meeting video from {{city}}'s portal, re-checks every external link on the site, and rebuilds the search index. This page reports what actually happened, including when it failed.

A site that quietly rots looks identical to one that is current, right up until somebody relies on it. So the status below is generated by the jobs themselves — it is not maintained by hand and cannot be made to look better than it is.

What each job is allowed to do

  • Agendas and meetings. Fetches only documents {{city}} has already published, from {{portal_vendor}} at {{portal_url}}. It never logs in, never submits anything, and never touches a page the city has not made public.
  • Link check. Re-fetches every external link this chapter publishes. Cities move pages, and a records-request link that 404s fails a reader at the exact moment they were about to act.
  • Search index. Rebuilds the site's index from the pages and the extracted agenda text, so a resident can search a phrase and land on the agenda that carried it.
  • Nothing writes to a government system. Records requests are sent by a person, deliberately, never by a timer.
