⚖️ Your Rights Under Oklahoma Law

The Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. § 24A.1 et seq.) guarantees public access to government records. You do NOT need to provide your name, address, or reason for requesting records. Government agencies must respond promptly and may only charge reasonable fees for document retrieval and copying. If a request is denied, the agency must cite the specific legal exemption.

🛡️ How We Protect You

Move Weight Foundation serves as the legal requester on all submissions. The government receives the request from our foundation — not from you. We never collect or store your identity. The only thing we keep is the content of your request and a random tracking ID so you can follow its progress. If the city pushes back, our foundation handles it. If they demand fees, the community can crowdfund them. You stay anonymous through the entire process.
