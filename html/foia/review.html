Miami open-records review

Move Weight Foundation / Expose Miami

Updated 24 November 2026. This page tracks every filed City of Miami Open Records Act request and what the City has delivered in response. As of November 24, 2026, we have received records for two subjects (Police Use-of-Force Incident Reports and Animal Control) while five July 9 cover-letter subjects and our November 2 request regarding 1645 B St NE remain awaiting city production.

Records received: 24 November 2026: three redacted June 2026 Miami PD use-of-force incident reports (Cases 2026-0709, 2026-0775, 2026-0815), a signed Personal Identifiers Redaction Statement citing 51 O.S. 24A.5 and 24A.8, and our stamped July 10 request form. August 13, August 28 & September 28, 2026: Animal Control policy manual, shelter intake/outcome records, FY 25-26 budget reference (p. 49), staffing/overtime reports, redacted call cards and incident reports, a redaction statement, and a refusal covering 11 withheld incident reports.

Privacy & verification note: although the City's November 24 redaction statement states that birthdates, addresses, and personal identifiers were redacted, Case 2026-0815 page 1 retained unredacted third-party personal identifiers. The raw packet is archived privately offline and is not posted publicly without complete redaction.

1. Police use-of-force incident reports (June 2026) — def17eeb2ea59c2f

All Miami Police Department incident reports for June 2026 involving use of force. Form logged by City Clerk 7/10/26 03:15pm.

Status (Fulfilled 24 Nov 2026): City Clerk delivered three redacted incident reports — Case 2026-0709 (June 1, Officer Eulitt, Taser drive-stun assisting BIA), Case 2026-0775 (June 16, Will Rogers Turnpike/I-44 pursuit, hostage incident, and officer-involved shooting), and Case 2026-0815 (June 25, Officer Vanatta, Taser 7 deployment assisting BIA/Miami Nation warrant) — plus Personal Identifiers Redaction Statement. No fee charged.

2. Animal Control policies and records — ad40efc713323c4f

Policies, area and citywide incident records, staffing, budget, overtime, shelter intake and outcomes.

Status (Partial response — City marked closed 28 Sep 2026): Records received August 13 (policies, shelter intake/outcome, budget p. 49 pointer), August 28 (staffing & overtime), and September 28 (call cards, redacted incident reports, redaction statement, refusal of 11 reports citing juvenile confidentiality). Foundation acknowledged receipt and requested status of remaining open requests on October 18, 2026.

Status (Sent 2 Nov 2026): Hand-delivery notice emailed to City Clerk Melissa Moore on November 2, 2026 with completed City form. Awaiting city response.
