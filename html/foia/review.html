Six Miami records requests

Move Weight Foundation / Expose Miami

Updated 26 October 2026. Our July 9 cover letter listed six City of Miami records requests. We have received records for one subject in three deliveries. No subject-specific production for the other five subjects was located in the correspondence reviewed.

Records received: August 13: Animal Control policy manual and shelter records; August 28: staffing and overtime; September 28: call cards, incident reports, a redaction statement and a refusal covering 11 incident reports. The city also returned the request form with each delivery and pointed to its published budget.

City status: the clerk expressly marked the Animal Control request closed on September 28. Earlier responses asked for clarification of the area-incident scope; a reply resolving that question was not located in the reviewed thread.

Next questions: confirm receipt and disposition for each request, identify what was searched and supplied, and explain the precise basis for the 11 withheld reports and whether nonidentifying portions can be released. A gap in this collection does not establish that no response was sent elsewhere.

1. Council minutes and resolutions

January 2026 forward, including the executive-session records described in the July cover letter.

Status: No subject-specific production located in the reviewed correspondence.

2. City social-media records and moderation

City-operated accounts, content and activity, hidden or deleted material, administrators, moderation records and policy.

Status: No subject-specific production located in the reviewed correspondence.

3. Animal Control policies and records

Policies, area and citywide incident records, staffing, budget, overtime, shelter intake and outcomes.

Status: Records received August 13, August 28 and September 28. City marked closed September 28; 11 incident reports withheld.

4. City-attorney employment and related records

Employment, position and compensation information, personnel-related records, DA referrals concerning citizen complaints and photo-handling records.

Status: No subject-specific production located in the reviewed correspondence.

5. Complete digital export of city social-media accounts

A full digital export; the Foundation offered a USB drive. This is a separate format request that overlaps the social-media records subject.

Status: No subject-specific production located in the reviewed correspondence.

6. Jones-Parker communications

Email, text and other electronic communications between Kyla Jones and Bless Parker from January 2025 through June 2026.

Status: No subject-specific production located in the reviewed correspondence.

What we want clarified

For each request: receipt date, custodian, search, records released, remaining records, clarification or fees needed, withholding basis and next action. The cover letter establishes the six subjects; it does not by itself establish delivery of every completed form. The review includes both the Foundation's emails and the city's replies.
