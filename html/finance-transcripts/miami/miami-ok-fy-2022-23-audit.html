Condition: The City’s internal controls over cash monitoring were lacking, as the City was not investigating reconciling items upon completion of the bank reconciliations. Cause and Effect: Although the City in most cases did reconcile its various cash accounts within trivial differences and reviews occurred, there were instances identified where follow up efforts in determining the cause of long outstanding reconciling items that had not yet cleared the bank. Recommendation: We recommend the city reviews its outstanding reconciling items timely and investigate differences that remain outstanding month to month.
