Mayor Bless Parker Evidence File: Vance Posts, Criminal Complaint, Public Records Questions

Updated May 31, 2026 | Sources: screenshots supplied from public Facebook posts, complaint pages, OML page captures, Miami/Ottawa public records leads

This page organizes the public screenshots and complaint pages now in the evidence file concerning Bless Parker, Mayor of Miami, Oklahoma. It separates what the screenshots show from what remains an allegation that needs to be matched against official records.

Evidence standard: Screenshots and complaint pages are leads, not court findings. The next step is matching each claim to city agendas, minutes, contracts, emails, ORA responses, social-media archives, law-enforcement case records, and employment/compensation records.

1. Official-Page Commercial Promotion Questions

Several provided screenshots show posts from pages labeled Bless Parker or Bless Parker Mayor of Miami Oklahoma promoting or sharing posts for John Vance Auto Group and related dealerships in Miami.

  • April 9, 2025: screenshot text says, "Let's Bless Your Ride! Employee Pricing at the Ford/Lincoln and Dodge/Jeep/Chrysler/Ram stores. Give me a call or text at 918-541-8839."
  • April 25, 2025: screenshot shows a Bless Parker post sharing a Vance Fleet Services item and referencing Vance Chevy Buick GMC of Miami, Vance Ford-Lincoln, and Vance Dodge Chrysler Jeep Ram.
  • May 9, 2025: screenshot shows a Bless Parker post congratulating a customer on a 2026 Chevy Equinox purchase and thanking them for staying local with Vance Chevy Buick.
  • May 23, 2025: screenshots show "Come see me or shoot me a text 918-541-8839" and "Give me a call or text 918-541-8839 and Let's Bless Your Ride! #blessyourride" attached to John Vance Auto Group material.

The public-records question is whether these posts were made on an official government page, whether they constitute public records, whether any were deleted after records requests or press inquiries, and whether the mayor's private employment or compensation created a conflict of interest.

2. Citizen Criminal Complaint Filed April 21, 2026

The complaint pages provided identify the complainant as Ronald Edward Durbin II of Guerrilla.News / Guerrilla Publishing and list the receiving agency as the Miami Police Department, City of Miami, Oklahoma. The complaint identifies the primary subject as Bless Parker, Mayor, City of Miami, Oklahoma, and lists bparker@miamiokla.net as the subject email.

The complaint identifies Parker's subject employer as John Vance Auto Group / Vance Chevrolet Buick GMC, 1601 N. Main Street, Miami, Oklahoma 74354. It alleges multiple categories of misconduct. These remain allegations unless confirmed by investigative findings or court records.

3. Counts And Statutes Alleged In The Complaint

  • Count One: alleged felony misuse of official position for personal financial gain, citing 21 O.S. 365, 21 O.S. 381, and 21 O.S. 341.
  • Count Two: alleged willful destruction of public records subject to the Oklahoma Open Records Act, citing 51 O.S. 24A.17(A), 51 O.S. 24A.3, and 21 O.S. 540.
  • Count Three: alleged willful disclosure of executive-session proceedings, citing 25 O.S. 307 and 25 O.S. 314.
  • Count Four: alleged official oppression, intimidation, and retaliation against private citizens and public employees, citing 21 O.S. 567 and First Amendment concerns.
  • Conspiracy allegation: the complaint also references 21 O.S. 341 in connection with alleged coordinated conduct.

4. Open Records And Deleted-Post Preservation Issue

The complaint states that on or about April 20, 2026, an Open Records Act request was served on the City of Miami seeking records associated with Mayor Parker's official government Facebook page, including posts, commercial solicitations, deletions, and related content.

It further alleges that after the ORA request and active reporting, one or more posts were deleted from the official "Mayor of Miami Oklahoma" Facebook page. The key record-preservation issue is whether Meta/Facebook records, city-device records, page admin logs, screenshots, and city email can confirm what existed, when it was deleted, and who controlled the page.

5. Executive Session And Retaliation Allegations

The complaint alleges recorded audio exists concerning direct communications between Durbin and Mayor Parker about Miami City Council executive-session subject matter. It also alleges retaliation or intimidation directed at people believed to be sources, including City Attorney Misty Fields Barnes.

Records to check include executive-session agenda items, required minutes or post-session records, council meeting video, public-comment records, city attorney employment records, text-message metadata, and the audio referenced in the complaint.

6. Personal-Life Screenshots

Some provided screenshots show public posts by Parker and Kendra Parker about private marital issues. This page does not repeat sensitive personal-health allegations. Those screenshots are relevant only to the extent they help establish a timeline of public statements, deleted posts, retaliation claims, or the authenticity/control of public Facebook pages.

7. Strip Club / Sade Bass Allegation

A provided public Facebook screenshot attributed to Kendra Parker states that it was "not a one night stand," names Sade Bass, alleges $3,600 was paid for Parker and four mayors to enter a champagne room, and asks what was received for that payment. This is an allegation from a screenshot. The records question is whether any public money, campaign money, municipal travel, official event, reimbursement, or other public nexus exists.

8. Ottawa County Jail Judgment Context

A supplied Miami News-Record screenshot says the Ottawa County Board of Commissioners scheduled a special meeting at 1 p.m. Tuesday, May 19, in the Civic Center banquet room at 129 5th Ave. in Miami, concerning the Austin Bond v. Sheriff of Ottawa County judgment.

The screenshot reports a $33 million verdict related to the 2015 death in custody of Terral Ellis II and says officials were expected to discuss financing options including a limited sales-tax-backed bond, a 10-year levy, a three-year unlimited ad valorem levy, or possible bankruptcy. The screenshot says no votes would be taken and that Miami News-Record would livestream the meeting.

9. OML Board And Public Contact Context

Provided screenshots from the Oklahoma Municipal League site list board meeting information at 201 N.E. 23rd Street, Oklahoma City, OK 73105, phone 405-528-7515, and show Bless Parker, Mayor, Miami as District 1. Another provided city contact screenshot lists the Miami mayor and council contacts, including Mayor Bless Parker at 918-542-9251.

10. Records To Pull Next

  1. Full Facebook/Meta preservation package for the "Mayor of Miami Oklahoma" page, including deleted posts, admin logs, timestamps, and page-role history.
  2. City of Miami ORA request and response logs for April 2026 requests involving Parker, Vance Auto Group, Facebook posts, city email, texts, and financial records.
  3. Employment, compensation, and conflict-disclosure records involving Parker and John Vance Auto Group.
  4. City council agendas, minutes, video, executive-session entries, and public comments for April 2026.
  5. Any Miami Police Department, Ottawa County Sheriff's Department, District Attorney, or OSBI case number or referral record connected to the complaint.
  6. Ottawa County commissioners meeting records and livestream/video for the special jail-judgment meeting.
