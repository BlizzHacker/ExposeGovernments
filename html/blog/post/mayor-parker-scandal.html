Mayor Parker Paid $3,600 for Strip Club Champagne Room — With 4 Other Mayors

April-May 2026 | Sources: Guerrilla Publishing OK, Instagram, Facebook

According to publicly available social media posts, Mayor Bless Parker reportedly paid $3,600 for himself and 4 other mayors to access a champagne room at an Oklahoma City strip club with 24-year-old performer "Sade Bass."

The Allegations

Multiple social media accounts have posted allegations about the incident. Public social media posts claim:

"Meet 24 year old 'STRIPPER' Sade Bass! Bless Parker you are a real politician now! Paid $3600 for him and 4 mayors to go back in a champagne room at the strip club..."

Public Sources

The allegations were first reported by Guerrilla Publishing OK and have been shared across multiple social media platforms including Instagram and Facebook.

Important Note: These allegations are based on public social media posts. ExposeMiamiOK does not independently verify the accuracy of third-party social media content. All information is presented as reported by original sources.

Questions Raised

  • Who were the other 4 mayors present?
  • Was taxpayer money used to fund this expense?
  • What was the official purpose of the meeting, if any?
  • Were proper disclosure and ethics requirements followed?

Ongoing Investigation

According to separate reports, criminal complaints have been filed with the Miami Police Department, and there are allegations that the FBI is investigating corruption involving Miami city officials. These investigations are ongoing.

Public Reaction

The allegations have sparked significant public discussion on social media and at city council meetings. Residents have raised concerns about ethical standards and accountability in local government.

This story continues to develop as more information becomes available through public records and official investigations.
