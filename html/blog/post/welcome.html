Welcome to ExposeMiamiOK — Why We Exist

May 29, 2026 | Editorial Team

Every community deserves watchdogs. Every government needs to know its citizens are paying attention. That's why ExposeMiamiOK exists.

The Mission

Miami, Oklahoma is a city with rich heritage, hardworking citizens, and enormous potential. But like any community, it faces challenges when those in power operate without sufficient scrutiny. This site was created to change that.

We believe in three core principles:

  • Transparency: Every public dollar, every public decision, should be accessible to every citizen.
  • Accountability: Public officials work for the people. When they fail to serve the public interest, citizens deserve to know.
  • Justice: Corruption, waste, and abuse of power should never go unchallenged.

What You'll Find Here

This site serves two purposes:

1. A Complete Resource Hub — We've mirrored and linked to every section of the official City of Miami website (miamiok.gov). Government pages, services, utilities, bids, agendas, minutes — it's all accessible from our resources section. Information should never be hard to find.

2. An Investigative Blog — Front and center. This is where we publish investigations, analysis, and commentary on local government actions. Budget breakdowns, contract examinations, policy analysis, and accountability reporting.

How You Can Help

We need your eyes, your ears, and your stories. If you've witnessed something that doesn't add up — a contract that seems rigged, spending that can't be justified, decisions made behind closed doors — we want to hear from you.

"The price of freedom is eternal vigilance." — Thomas Jefferson

Submit tips to tips@exposemiamiok.com. All sources are protected. Your identity will never be revealed without your explicit consent.

What's Next

This is just the beginning. We have investigations in progress, public records requests filed, and stories to tell. Stay tuned, share this site with your neighbors, and together we'll make Miami, Oklahoma a model of transparent governance.

The citizens of Miami deserve better. And we're here to make sure they get it.
