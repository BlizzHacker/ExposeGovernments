$33 Million Verdict Upheld: Ottawa County Jail Death of Terral Ellis Jr.

📅 April 2026 📖 Wrongful Death ⏱️ 5 min read

The 10th Circuit Court of Appeals upheld a $33 million verdict against Ottawa County in the wrongful death of Terral Ellis Jr., age 26, who died of sepsis and pneumonia while in custody. Video evidence shows Ellis begging for help for 20 hours before his death. The judgment has grown to approximately $40 million with interest — a burden that falls on Ottawa County taxpayers.
