Records first

Rick Aldridge and Miami’s charity network

A public-record map of Oklahoma Emergency Response Team, the Good Neighbor names, civic appearances, public support, and the documents still needed to understand how the organizations relate.

Published September 13, 2026. Sources are linked at the claim they support.

What the current record establishes:

Rick Aldridge has publicly represented Oklahoma Emergency Response Team and appeared in official Miami and Ottawa County records involving community programs. Two named organizations are currently listed by the IRS as tax-exempt public charities. The sources reviewed for this page do not establish wire fraud or another crime.

Entity check

Which names have a verified legal identity?

Program names, Facebook pages, corporations, and IRS-exempt organizations are not interchangeable. Each has to be matched through a filing, an EIN, or an official record.

IRS-listed public charity

Oklahoma Emergency Response Team Inc.

EIN 46-5454378. The IRS Oklahoma exempt-organization file lists the organization with subsection 03 status, a July 2014 ruling date, and a December 2025 tax period. Its filing code indicates Form 990-N treatment.

IRS exempt-organization file
IRS-listed public charity

Good Neighbor Coalition

EIN 93-3781759. The same IRS file lists this Miami organization with subsection 03 status, an April 2024 ruling date, and a December 2025 tax period. It also has a Form 990-N filing code.

Money and public support

Amounts that can be documented

Private foundation grant

$30,000 to OKERT

Tulsa Community Foundation’s public 2020 Form 990 Schedule I reports a $30,000 cash grant to Oklahoma Emergency Response Team Inc., EIN 46-5454378, for general operations.

Foundation Form 990 public disclosure
City fee waiver

$13,500 stated value for Santa program

A city cover sheet describes the financial implication of waiving facility fees as a $13,500 donation to the event. This is evidence of foregone fees, not evidence that the city paid $13,500 in cash. The request names Cori Stotts and the Chuck Stotts Big Santa Program.

Official city packet

A 990-N filing does not provide a public itemized income-and-expense statement. The absence of a full Form 990 is not, by itself, evidence that an organization failed to file or committed fraud.

Open questions

What still needs proof

  • Are the Good Neighbor Project, Good Neighbor Coalition, and OKERT separate operations, fiscal-sponsor relationships, or names used for the same program at different times?
  • Which entity received each donation solicited through program names or social-media pages, and which bank or payment account was disclosed to donors?
  • What written agreements governed the facility described in the December 23, 2025 reader-supplied Facebook screenshot?
  • Did Miami, Ottawa County, tribal governments, or other public bodies provide grants, fee waivers, property, utilities, equipment, or contracts to any of the named entities?
  • What board, conflict-of-interest, restricted-gift, and financial-control policies did each legal entity maintain during the relevant period?
  • What is the legal identity, if any, of Ottawa County Community Connections and the Chuck Stotts Big Santa Program?

Document plan

Records to obtain next

  1. IRS: exemption applications and determination correspondence for EINs 46-5454378 and 93-3781759, plus available 990-N e-postcard history.
  2. Oklahoma Secretary of State: certificates, amendments, registered-agent history, and status records for both exact legal names.
  3. Oklahoma Attorney General: charitable-solicitation registrations, exemptions, annual reports, complaints subject to disclosure, and enforcement records for the named entities.
  4. City of Miami: agreements, waivers, permits, inspections, property or utility support, grants, purchase orders, and communications mentioning Aldridge, OKERT, Good Neighbor Project, Good Neighbor Coalition, or the Santa program.
  5. Ottawa County: the same categories, including emergency-management support and spring-cleanup partnership records.
  6. Organizations: voluntarily request board rosters, conflict policy, annual budgets, restricted-fund accounting, and donation-channel reconciliation. Clearly label any response voluntary rather than a public-record obligation.

Method and limits

How this file is built

Official government minutes, official city packets and video, IRS data, and the grantmaker’s own tax filing receive the most weight. A social-media screenshot is a lead until its original post, account control, date, and surrounding context are preserved. Similar names are not merged without a filing or another reliable identity link.

We sought evidence that could disconfirm the concern as well as evidence that could support further inquiry. The current IRS status and grant record are included because omitting them would give readers a distorted picture.
