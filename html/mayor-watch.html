⚠️ WHY WE'RE HERE

This isn't a fan page. This isn't "government transparency" gift-wrapped in polite language. We live in Miami, Oklahoma, and we're done watching our city get hollowed out by people who think nobody's paying attention.

Mayor Bless Parker runs two Facebook profiles. He has outside employment he doesn't disclose. He deletes content from city pages the moment it makes him look bad — we have the screenshots from before the delete key landed. His divorce records are public. We pulled them. All of it is archived here because once something hits this page, it doesn't disappear just because city hall got embarrassed.

When an 11-year-old girl got mauled by a dog and animal control did nothing — not a follow-up, not an investigation, not a goddamn thing — that was the moment a lot of us stopped pretending this was about "policy disagreements." That's negligence with a city seal on it. The city attorney retaliates against anyone who asks hard questions. City council meetings are theater. And Parker sits at the center of all of it, collecting a paycheck and counting on everyone looking the other way.

Ron Durbin and Guerrilla Publishing have been in this fight longer than most of us. They're local. They're loud. They're right. We work alongside them because sunlight is the only thing that actually works — and Miami, Oklahoma is long overdue for some.

Here's how it works: we scrape Facebook posts before they get deleted. We file FOIA requests the city hates answering. We screenshot everything. We archive everything. This page is the public record the city refuses to hand over voluntarily — built by Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. § 24A.1) demands and a lot of pissed-off citizens who decided waiting on accountability was a losing game.
