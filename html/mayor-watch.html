⚠️ ABOUT MAYOR WATCH

Mayor Bless Parker was elected to lead Miami, Oklahoma. Mayor Watch is a citizen-powered oversight project that monitors every public action, every city council vote, every budget decision, and every public statement made by the Mayor and his administration.

This project uses Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. § 24A.1) to obtain public documents, meeting recordings, financial records, and communications. All evidence is public record and published for citizen review.

⚠️ If you have evidence of misconduct, waste, fraud, or abuse by Mayor Parker or any Miami city official — submit it anonymously here.
