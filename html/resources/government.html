Members

The City of Miami supports a City Council / City Manager form of government. In Miami, the Mayor and City Council serve part-time and are elected on a non-partisan basis. Under the Council / Manager form of government, the City Council acts as the legislative body in establishing policy and law and the City Manager handles the day-to-day management of the City organization.

Miami is divided into four wards with an even population distribution. The City Council consists of one member from each ward and a Mayor. Each member is eligible to serve three years, with elections held every year. Elections are rotated with either two council positions or the mayor up for reelection.

City Attorney

View on miamiok.gov →

Overview

The primary responsibility of the City Attorney is the protection of the legal interests of the City of Miami. The City Attorney serves as general counsel to the City and provides advice to the Mayor and Council, City Boards and Commissions, City Administration and City Departments.

The City Attorney's Office cannot give legal advice or provide legal representation to private citizens.

City Manager

View on miamiok.gov →

Overview

The City of Miami functions under a City Council / City Manager form of government in which the City Manager is appointed by the City Council and reports directly to the elected officials. As the City's Chief Administrative Officer (CAO), the City Manager is responsible for carrying out policy enacted by the Council, overseeing the administration of the City, and maintaining inter-governmental relationships.

Overview

Welcome to MIO Airport! We're thrilled to have you here and invite you to enjoy our complimentary coffee and water, available for both pilots and guests. To make your visit even more enjoyable, we offer courtesy cars so you can explore the charming town of Miami, Oklahoma. Discover fantastic local businesses and experience the warm hospitality that awaits you. Whether you're here for a short layover or an extended stay, we hope you make the most of your time with us.

Whether you're wandering through the historic downtown, enjoying a performance at the Coleman Theatre, or savoring local cuisine, you'll find plenty of opportunities to make lasting memories. Our community prides itself on making visitors feel welcome, and we are eager to share our rich history, scenic landscapes, and friendly faces with you. Come and discover why Miami, Oklahoma, is a place where you’ll always feel at home.

The Miami Regional Airport, situated in the northwest area of Miami, Oklahoma, spans approximately 300 acres and holds significant potential for becoming a hub of aeronautical development. As the aviation industry continues to expand, regional airports like Miami Regional can play a crucial role in fostering economic growth and innovation within their communities. By focusing on aeronautical development, the airport could attract a range of aviation-related businesses, from aircraft maintenance and repair facilities to flight training schools and aerospace manufacturing companies. This would not only create jobs and boost the local economy but also enhance the airport's infrastructure and connectivity. By investing in modern facilities and collaborating with stakeholders, Miami Regional Airport has the opportunity to transform into a dynamic center for aviation excellence.

With its 28 rentable hangars and a jet pad, the airport is well-equipped to serve a range of aviation needs. The asphalt runway, designated 17/35, stretches 5,020 feet in length and 100 feet in width, offering ample space for takeoff and landing operations. This runway supports a load capacity of up to 22,000 lbs for single axle and 44,000 lbs for double axle aircraft, making it suitable for accommodating a variety of larger aircraft. This combination of facilities not only ensures efficient operations but also offers flexibility for different types of aircraft and aviation activities, catering to both private and commercial aviation demands.

We also cater to all your fueling and ground service needs with both Avgas and JetA available for self-service or full-service options. We proudly accept major credit cards and FuelMaster, ensuring a seamless transaction process.

Thank you for visiting us! Whether your visit was for business or leisure, we're thrilled to have had the opportunity to connect with you. We hope you found what you were looking for and enjoyed your time here. Please don't hesitate to return soon—our doors are always open, and we'd love to welcome you back.

Safe travels until we meet again!

The Application Process

The City of Miami accepts applications only for jobs that are currently open.

Job descriptions and applications are included with each job posting. You can also obtain a job description and application at the Human Resource Department of the City of Miami between the hours of 8 a.m. to 5 P.M., Monday through Friday.

The completed application and current resume must be submitted by the closing date and time as stated in the job opening.

All job openings are listed on the City of Miami's website. We also list job openings with Work Force Oklahoma, NEO A&M College, and the City of Miami's Facebook page.

The Department of Risk Management coordinates the receipt, investigation, and resolution of liability claims filed against the city.
