What Happened To The Old Posts?

Older Facebook post pages may still exist as internal capture records, but the public archive now points here so visitors see the moderation policy first.

For public use, ExposeMiamiOK should publish records, official links, meeting clips, and reviewed summaries. For staff use, the admin dashboard can still review the raw queue and decide whether a lead deserves a FOIA request or public record note.
