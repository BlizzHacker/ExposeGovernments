Request for Approval

sworn officers.
The Miami Police Department has maintained a patrol rifle program for many years.
This proposal seeks to replace the aging and outdated patrol rifles that were traded
during the department's handgun replacement project and to increase the number of
rifles available for patrol operations.
The acquisition of additional rifles will improve officer readiness, enhance public safety,
and ensure sufficient patrol rifle availability across all patrol shifts. The funding is
already in the current budget.

Background

In 2025, the Miami Police Department traded several older patrol rifles as part of a
department-wide firearms modernization project that resulted in the acquisition of
updated duty handguns. While that project successfully improved the department's
handgun inventory, it reduced the number of patrol rifles available for deployment.

The department currently maintains an established patrol rifle program, including written
policies, qualified instructors, and trained officers. This proposal does not create a new
capability; it restores and expands an existing one by acquiring modern, reliable rifle
systems that meet current law enforcement standards and align with the police
department's current operational platforms.

Operational Need

Patrol rifles remain an essential law enforcement tool for responding to:
      Active shooter incidents
      Armed and barricaded suspects
      High-risk felony calls
      School safety threats
      Incidents involving suspects armed with rifles

      Critical infrastructure protection

handgun, particularly at extended distances. Modern patrol rifles enhance officers'
ability to stop deadly threats while reducing the risk of unintended harm to bystanders
through improved accuracy and accountability.

Increasing the number of available rifles will also ensure that more patrol officers have
immediate access to a rifle when circumstances require one, reducing delays
associated with sharing equipment between shifts and units.
