C. Discussion and Possible Action Regarding Joplin Crusaders Using the Multi-Sports Complex/Red Robertson Field
Sam Sturgis, owner, operator, and head coach of the Joplin Crusaders would like the team to play games at the Multi-
Sports Complex/Red Robertson Field. Currently they play in Coffeyville, Kansas. Sturgis explained that with players
coming in from the region, this could bring in tax revenue. Players give back to the community through volunteer service.
Sturgis owns several businesses and obtains sponsorships to cover the expenses of the program. The season begins in
March and ends in July and games are Saturday evenings. Sturgis would need to know in December if it would be possible
for them to play at the stadium. They provide a one (1) day youth football camp in June. Sturgis would need a place for
players to change, scoreboard, and chains. The Crusaders contract to provide security, and the Trust would need an
agreement with the contracted security provider. Discussion will continue with staff and this will be a future agenda
item.

     A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE MIAMI COMMUNITY FACILITIES
                   AUTHORITY OF THE CITY OF MIAMI, OKLAHOMA,
                   ESTABLISHING BUDGET AMENDMENT AUTHORITY

        WHEREAS, the Trust Indenture of the Miami Community Facilities Authority (Indenture)
provides for a trust purpose at Article IV, paragraph 7, and a matching trust power at Article VIII,
paragraph 15, as follows: "To expend all funds coming into the hands of the Trustees as revenue or
otherwise for the payment of any indebtedness incurred by the Trustees for the purposes specified
herein, and in the payment of the aforesaid costs and expenses, and in payment of any other obligation
properly chargeable against the Trust Estate, ... "; and

         WHEREAS, the Trust Indenture of the Miami Community Facilities Authority provides the
following trust powers at Article VIII, paragraphs 29 and 30: "To exercise exclusive management and
control of the properties of the Trust Estate; [t]o contract for the furnishing of any services or the
performance of any duties that they may deem necessary or proper, and pay for the same as they see
fit"; and

        WHEREAS, the Trust Indenture of the Miami Community Facilities Authority provides at Article
VII, paragraph 6, that the City Manager of the City of Miami, Oklahoma, serves ex officio as General
Manager and Executive Director for the Trust Estate, and that he or she is charged with " ... the
authority ... to manage Trust facilities and activities in a manner consistent with the policies established
by written resolution of the Board of Trustees ... "; and

         WHEREAS, the Trust Indenture of the Miami Community Facilities Authority provides at Article
VIII, paragraph 28, that the Board of Trustees may " ... do any and all things necessary or convenient to
carry out its purposes and exercise the powers given and granted herein, and to do all other acts in their
judgment necessary or desirable, for the property and advantageous management, investment and
distribution of the Trust Estate and income therefrom"; and

      WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees desires to establish budget amendment authority to the Trust
Manager; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE MIAMI COMMUNITY
FACILITIES AUTHORITY AS FOLLOWS:

    1. The Miami Community and Facilities Authority of the City of Miami, OK, pursuant to the
       Indenture, does hereby authorize the Trust Manager to transfer any unexpended and
       unencumbered appropriations, at any time throughout FY 2026-2027, from one line item to
       another, one object category to another within a department, or one department to another
       within a fund, and one fund to another fund without further approval by the Trust; and

    2. The Trust Manager shall be obligated to make report of actions taken under this authority at the
       next regular meeting following said actions; and

    3. This Resolution may be revoked or amended at any time by vote of a majority of a quorum of
       the Board of Trustees at a regular or special meeting, but such revocation or amendment shall
