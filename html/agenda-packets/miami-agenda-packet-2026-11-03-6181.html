Call to Order

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances

Discuss and Approve September 2026 Meeting Minutes:
Board Member Jones motioned to approve the September Minutes. Board Member Frazier made the

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Frisbie: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye, Chenoweth: Absent

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Main 66 Venue Acoustics Discussion:
Trinda Crow recognized Zeb Mingori to talk about the acoustics of Main 66 Venue. There has been issues
brought up about the noise level in the venue. Some options that were discussed were relocating the
HVAC system to the storage room which would put it behind a wall, but cost around 7,350$. It could be
moved on the roof, but would potentially damage the new roof. This also raises the question would we
need to move it back if the building sells. Some other solutions for the HVAC system would be changing
the blower speed, or adding some vents. That would allow the air to leave more spread out and not so
forcefully. Carpet runners or rugs would help the sound, and could get a quote for carpet squares. Would
also need to keep cleaning in mind with them. Main Street could find a fundraiser to do to help. Plans
moving forward are getting quotes for carpet squares, finding someone with an acoustics background to
look at the building for suggestions, or even draping fabric from the ceiling to catch sound, while still
keeping the look of the venue. We could do the entire length of the building or if we need to back off we
can.

None

Approval of 2027 MMS Event Calendar:
The Main Street Board began planning the 2027 calendar for Main Street in September and put some
dates forth to be voted on in this meeting. The big changes are keeping After Hours on Main, combining
Sip and Shops with the food truck night that happens then. This also includes Junktique on September
11, The Heart of Main Street Dinner on Sept 25, Sweet Street on Oct 29, and the Holiday Market on
November 12-13.

Board Member Frisbie motioned to approve the 2027 Calendar. Board Member Jones made the second.
The board was polled with the following results:

Frisbie: Aye, Jones: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye, Frazier: Absent: Chenoweth: Absent

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.
