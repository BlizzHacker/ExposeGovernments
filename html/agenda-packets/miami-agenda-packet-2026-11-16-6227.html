The agenda was filed in the office of the City Clerk and posted in the main hallway and the north outside

2026, pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) and (b).

THE TRUST MAY DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE ON ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

Call to Order
Chairman Forkum called the meeting to order at 4:00 PM.

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances
None.

Consent Agenda
By unanimous consent the public body may designate noncontroversial items to be considered in one
motion and one vote. The public body may add items from the regular agenda and approve. Posted
agenda items not added to the consent docket will be considered separately in their regular order. Staff
recommends items 4 and 5 be placed on the consent agenda.

Coleman Managing Director’s Report
Danny Dillon gave the Coleman report. It is a busy time and the Route 66 centennial has helped with
that. In September there was 4 lunches on stage, 4 dinners, 3 ballroom events, plenty of tours with
some groups, and a showing of the Rt 66 documentary. October is looking just as busy with 4 lunches, 2
dinners, 7 shows, and 2 groups scheduled for tours. There has not been great interest in Halloween.

Main Street Update
Jesse Black reported on Main Street. There is a Sip and Shop on October 27, and then the 30th is Sweet
Street. Following that the Holiday Market will be on November 13-14. The Centennial committee is also
having a Route 66 Birthday Party on the 10th in the Main 66 Venue. After that there is a Sip and Shop
December 17th.
