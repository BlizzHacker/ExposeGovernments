THE MIAMI COMMUNITY FACILITIES AUTHORITY (MCFA) MET IN SPECIAL SESSION AUGUST 19, 2026, IN THE NEO A&M
CROSSLAND CENTER COMPLEX AT 11:00AM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Kyle Stafford, Chairman                                        Tyler Cline, Trust Manager
Jill Douthit, Trustee                                          Melissa Moore, City Clerk
Bless Parker, Trustee
Mark Zordel, Trustee
Dustin Grover, Trustee
Brandon Fletcher, Trustee
Shannon Duhon, Trustee (Absent)

Notice of a special meeting of the Miami Community Facilities Authority, Miami, Oklahoma, called by Chairman Dr. Kyle
Stafford, was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and by posting on www.miamiok.gov. starting at
9:40AM on August 17, 2026, pursuant to 25 O.S. §311(9) (a) and (b) and the agenda was displayed and posted in the
same manner on August 18, 2026, at 10:00AM.

THE TRUST MAY TAKE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING ACTIONS: DISCUSS, CONSIDER AND VOTE FOR APPROVAL,
ACCEPTANCE, REJECTION, AMENDMENT AND/OR POSTPONEMENT OF ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

1. Call to Order
Chairman Stafford called the meeting to order at 11:18AM.

2. Discussion Items

A. Discussion on the Financial Report
The monthly financial report was provided in their packet. Chairman Stafford announced that CFO Crystal Wyrick had
reached out regarding the question about the prior year’s carryover amount. Wyrick reported that the figure Jill
Fitzgibbon provided during the budget presentation was correct.

No action taken.

3. Discussion and Possible Action Items

A. Discussion and Possible Action on the Claims List for Fiscal Year 2025-2026
Trustee Parker left the meeting at 11:19AM.

Trustee Grover made a motion to approve the claims list for fiscal year 2025-2026. Trustee Zordel made the second. The
Trust was polled with the following results:

Grover, Aye   Zordel, Aye     Fletcher, Aye   Douthit, Aye Parker, Absent Stafford, Aye      Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

B. Discussion and Possible Action on the Claims List for Fiscal Year 2026-2027
Trustee Zordel made a motion to approve the claims list for fiscal year 2026-2027. Trustee Fletcher made the second.
The Trust was polled with the following results:

Zordel, Aye   Fletcher, Aye    Grover, Aye    Douthit, Aye Parker, Absent Stafford, Aye      Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

C. Discussion and Possible Action on the Minutes: June 24, 2026 (Special Meeting)
Trustee Zordel made a motion to approve minutes of the June 24, 2026, special meeting. Chairman Stafford made the

Trustee Parker entered the meeting at 11:23AM.

D. Resolution MCFA 2026-01 Establishing Budget Amendment Authority for Fiscal Year 2026-2027
Wyrick stated that the resolution matches what the Council approved on the city side regarding the City Manager’s
budget amendment authority.

Chairman Stafford made a motion to approve Resolution MCFA 2026-01 Establishing Budget Amendment Authority for
Fiscal Year 2026-2027. Trustee Zordel made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Stafford, Aye    Zordel, Aye    Parker, Aye Fletcher, Aye          Grover, Aye    Douthit, Aye Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

E. Discussion and Possible Action on the Memorandum of Understanding With the Miami Youth Football League for
Fiscal Year 2026-2027
Trustee Grover explained that this agreement and the agreement with the Northeastern Oklahoma Soccer Association
are the same as prior years.

Trustee Parker made a motion to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with the Miami Youth Football League
for fiscal year 2026-2027. Trustee Douthit made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Parker, Aye Douthit, Aye Grover, Aye          Fletcher, Aye       Zordel, Aye    Stafford, Aye   Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

F. Discussion and Possible Action on the Memorandum of Understanding With Northeastern Oklahoma Soccer
Association for Fiscal Year 2026-2027
Trustee Grover explained that they use the field for some practices.

Trustee Douthit made a motion to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with Northeastern Oklahoma Soccer
Association for fiscal year 2026-2027. Trustee Fletcher made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Douthit, Aye Fletcher, Aye      Grover, Aye     Parker, Aye Zordel, Aye          Stafford, Aye   Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

G. Discussion and Possible Action on the Video Board Operations Agreement With NEO A&M College Jumbotron Team
Chairman Stafford advised that the jumbotron team will use the two (2) cameras option ($750) again this season.

Trustee Douthit made a motion to approve the video board operations agreement with NEO A&M College Jumbotron
Team. Trustee Fletcher made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Douthit, Aye Fletcher, Aye      Grover, Abstain      Parker, Aye Zordel, Aye        Stafford, Abstain   Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

H. Discussion and Possible Action on Daktronics 5 Year Scoreboard Service Agreement
Trustees determined that would be more cost effective to pay the lump sum option versus the 5-year annual payment
option.

Trustee Parker made a motion to approve the Daktronics 5-year scoreboard service agreement with the amendment
that MCFA is the purchaser and payment to be made in one lump sum. Trustee Fletcher made the second. The Trust
was polled with the following results:

Parker, Aye Fletcher, Aye   Grover, Aye   Douthit, Aye Zordel, Aye   Stafford, Aye   Duhon, Absent

Chairman Stafford declared the motion approved.

4. Chairman Remarks and Discussion of the Upcoming Football Season
Chairman Stafford thanked the suiteholders for attending the suite holders’ luncheon and for continuing to be
supporters of the complex. Chairman Stafford also introduced the Trustees of MCFA and announced the following
improvements to the stadium: new scoreboard, access control, the new turf. Coaches from Miami High School and NEO
A & M College discussed their upcoming season. Chairman Stafford went over some housekeeping information for the
suiteholders such as the deadline for promotional videos, access control, and stocking their suite for the games.

No action taken.

5. Adjournment
Trustee Grover moved to adjourn. Trustee Fletcher made the second.

Chairman Stafford declared the meeting adjourned at 12:49PM.
