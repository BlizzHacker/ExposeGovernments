A. Discussion on the City of Miami Dilapidated Structure Demolition Checklist and Procedures
Travis Jones explained the process for declaring and demolishing a dilapidated structure. There is a checklist that
is included in the packet that staff use during the process. The steps for demolishing a dilapidated structure include
the initial report, reviewing and making a determination on if it qualifies per state statute, completing an
inspection report, scheduling a property hearing, preparing and the delivery of the required notices, conducting
a property hearing, making official findings, issuing a written order, and filing the notice of lien with the County
Clerk. Jones explained that if the City of Miami does not follow the procedures, then they can be sued. If the
property owner sells the property during this process, it can delay the process. If the property owner believes they
can fix the property, the city will work with them for an agreed upon amount of time. The city can move forward
with demolition if the property owner does not complete the project during the agreed upon time. Once the city
has posted the notice on the structure then the city has met their obligation. The owner is responsible for any
accidents on the property. The city can order the structure to be boarded and secured, and the owner will be
billed. The city budgets fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00) each year to remove dilapidated structures.
