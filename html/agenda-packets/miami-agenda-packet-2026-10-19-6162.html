Moved to the consent agenda.
Financial Report
Sherry White gave a financial report for the Coleman. The numbers appear low or trending negative, but
shows are beginning to kick off for the fiscal year. These numbers are just from August, and do not show
the entire picture.

none
Coleman Managing Director’s Report
Danny Dillon gave the Coleman report. Not too much new, shows are on sale now, and groups have
been steady coming in. The Coleman is remaining busy until Christmas. Last Saturday, September 19,
The Coleman showed a documentary on Route 66. It is to be shown on PBS after the tour. The Miami
section was good. The documentary was filmed early 2025. Guests came to watch from at least 10
states. Concerning the shops, Cultivate and Create is now open, and there has been a new project
concerning the tile work. Friends of the Coleman voted to cover the bill.

none
Main Street Update
Jesse Black reported on Main Street. Main Street had the first ever 66 Junktique Market. Numbers from
placer were not ready yet, but it had good attendance. The next major event downtown is Bull Bash,
and Main Street is getting ready for Sweet Street and Holiday Market.

none
Other New Business, if any, Which has Arisen since the Posting of the Agenda and Could Not Have
Been Anticipated Prior to the Time of Posting
Danny stated since Sweet Street is on the 30th, The Coleman has been considering the idea of having a
Halloween Night at the Coleman planned. They are checking for interest.

Trustee Community Announcements

Adjournment
Trustee Jones made a motion to adjourn. Trustee Stotts made the second. The Trust was polled with the
following results:

Jones: Aye, Stotts: Aye, Morgan: Aye, Chairman Forkum: Aye, Reese: Absent, Boyd: Absent, Estep: Absent

Chairman Forkum declared the motion carried. Adjourned at 4:13 P.M.
