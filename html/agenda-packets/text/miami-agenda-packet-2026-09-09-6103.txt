            MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
                  OF THE MIAMI SENIORS’ CENTER, AUGUST 12, 2026

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of The Miami Civic Center and on the front door
of The Miami Seniors’ Center and by posting on www.miamiokla.net pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) (b).

    1. On August 12th, 2026, The Miami Seniors Center Board of Directors met for their
       monthly meeting. The meeting was called to order at 10:03 am, by President Mary
       Susan Whaley with the following members answering present at roll call.

                   Kevin Browning, Member
                   Mark McDowell, Secretary
                   Mary Susan Whaley, President
                   Kathy Wright, Treasurer
                   Callie Cortner, MSC Director

                   Absent: Vacant Position

    2. Public Input or Appearances- None

    3. *Action Item* Approval of June Minutes After discussion of the minutes, Director
       Wright made a motion to approve the minutes as read. Director Browning a second
       to the motion. President Whaley called for a vote:

         Browning, Aye McDowell, Aye           Whaley, Aye       Wright, Aye      VACANCY

                   President Whaley declared motion carried.

    4. Callie gave a Director’s report. The printed report is attached to the minutes. We
       have a new 12 foot “EVENT TODAY” flag that will draw attention on days of events.
       There will be a dance and food available from Sticky Whiskers BBQ. It was also in
       the report that the Senior Center will not be accepting United Way Funds this
       coming year. DOCs will begin doing their own ordering and will give a report
       monthly of the numbers served. Meals on wheels will still be available and billed at
       the end of the month. Caitlyn reported that the names that will be submitted to the
       City Council for nomination to the Senior Center Board will be Eve Schnakenberg
       and Lisa Yates.


   5. There was no other business that needed to be discussed
