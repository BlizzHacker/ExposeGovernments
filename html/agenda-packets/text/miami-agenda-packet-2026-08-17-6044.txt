Resolution MDRA 2026-01 Establishing Budget Amendment Authority for Fiscal Year 2026-2027
Jill Fitzgibbon presented on establishing Budget Amendment Authority. The city has 31 funds which
includes MDRA Establishing Budget amendment Authority for the City Manager would allow him to
move funds between the 31 funds of the city, but would require him to report to the trust. This would
allow for transfers to be smoother.

Trustee Jones moved to approve the City Manager to have authority to move funding. Trustee Stotts
made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Stotts: Aye, Estep: Aye, Chairman Forkum: Aye Reese: Absent, Morgan: Absent, Boyd: Absent

Chairman Forkum declared the motion carried.

Discussion and Vote on Recommendation from MDRA Committee to Approve Lease of Coleman Shop
103
Chairman Forkum presented on behalf of the lease committee. The committee had interviews and is
recommending Cultivate and Create as the tenant for Coleman Shop 103. It’s mission is to be a
gathering place for local goods. It was presented by the owners of Flourchild Farms. In the committees
opinion, they had the best presentation. They would fall under the Free Food Act as they would be
making food at an inspected kitchen and packaged to sell at the shop.

Trustee Stotts moved to approve Cultivate and Create as the new tenant for 103 N Main. Trustee Jones
made the second. The Trust was polled with the following results:

Stotts: Aye, Jones: Aye, Estep: Aye, Chairman Forkum: Aye Reese: Absent, Morgan: Absent, Boyd: Absent

Chairman Forkum declared the motion carried.



Discuss and Vote to Approve Artist Engagements
Chairman Forkum gave the report on behalf of Danny Dillon. Dillon presented on Michael Martin
Murphy and now has a contract. City Manager Kline recommended voting pending legal review. He has
a large following and a large Christmas following. The contract needs to be signed by August 25 if he is
to perform at the Coleman. Due to no legal review and having time to make the decision, the trust
decided to table the motion until the next meeting.
