Main Street Coordinator Report:
Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Trinda Crow, Gretchen Frazier, and Black attended
Main Street Day at the Capital on May 4th. We have a Sip & Shop May 5th, and then we will be planting
flowers on Main Street tomorrow on May 6th. In June the CVB team will be working both the 8-Man All
Star Game, and the Mayor’s Conference. The Main Street Dinner was moved to June 4th. Jesse will be
attending director training through Oklahoma Main Street June 2-4 in Ardmore. Black asked the board if
there would be a conflict with having a special meeting in June on Monday, instead of the usual Tuesday
due to the training. No conflicts were given.

None
