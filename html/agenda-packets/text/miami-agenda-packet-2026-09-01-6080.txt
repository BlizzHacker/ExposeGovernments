D. Discussion and Possible Vote to Award Bid to Vanguard Utility Service, Inc. for Request For Bid
(RFB) 26-09 Miami Special Utility Authority (MSUA) Water Meter Installation Project and Authorize
the Trust Manager to Negotiate the Contract Consistent With the Bid Documents, and Contingent
Upon Legal Review. Both Bidder and MSUA Recognize That Many of the Units to be Provided Under
the Bid and Agreement Between Bidder and MSUA are to be Provided on a Unit Pricing Basis. In
Addition, Both Bidder and MSUA Recognize the Actual Number of Units to be Provided by Bidder are
Currently Only an Estimate. As a Result, the Agreement Between MSUA and Bidder Will Contain
Specific Language to Confirm That the Total Scope of the Project to be Provided by Bidder on a Unit
Pricing Basis may Increase or Decrease in the Sole Discretion of MSUA
Derric Lollar explained that ten (10) bids were received but only four (4) bids included all the requested
documents. Vanguard Utility Service was the highest ranked vendor on the weighted score. The bid was
open for twenty-one (21) days. The work to be completed with this bid is the water meter installation
which includes changing the meter, possibly the lid and/or the meter can. Waterline material inventory
will be completed during this project as well. No do not exceed amount is included in this because there
is a need to be flexible with the amounts as the work is completed. The reviews of the recommended
company are good. The project to install the meters will take six (6) to nine (9) months, and meters can
be read manually while the project is being completed. The meters are under warranty and run on a
battery. The battery is not replaceable but under a ten (10) year warranty. Trustee Jones commended
Lollar and the team for the weighted score sheet. Automatic re-reads can be done, and the customer will
still be able to read their own water meter. The accuracy of the new meters could cause the Trust to see
an increase in the number of gallons sold and will catch all the low flows. Trustee Estep requested that a
certificate of calibration be obtained for the meters.

Trustee Jones made a motion to approve awarding the bid to Vanguard Utility Service, Inc. for Request
for Bid (RFB) 26-09 Miami Special Utility Authority (MSUA) Water Meter Installation Project and authorize
the Trust Manager to negotiate the contract consistent with the bid documents, and contingent upon
legal review. Both Bidder and MSUA recognize that many of the units to be provided under the bid and
agreement between Bidder and MSUA are to be provided on a unit pricing basis. In addition, both Bidder
and MSUA recognize the actual number of units to be provided by bidder are currently only an estimate.
As a result, the agreement between MSUA and Bidder will contain specific language to confirm that the
total scope of the project to be provided by bidder on a unit pricing basis may increase or decrease in the
sole discretion of MSUA. The motion was seconded by Trustee Estep. The Trust was polled with the
following results:

Jones, Aye                Estep, Aye             Dunkel, Aye     Parker, Aye        Barnes, Absent

Chairman Parker declared the motion carried.
