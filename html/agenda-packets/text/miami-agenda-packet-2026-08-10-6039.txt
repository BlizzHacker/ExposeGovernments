THE AIRPORT ADVISORY BOARD MET IN REGULAR SESSION ON MAY 11, 2026, IN THE MIAMI REGIONAL AIRPORT
TERMINAL BUILDING AT 4:00PM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Pat Jurgensmeyer, Chairperson                                           Travis Jones, Airport Director
Jeff Stewart, Board Member                                              Jill Fitzgibbon, Airport Ofc Coordinator
Kyle Stafford, Board Member                                             Misty Barnes, City Attorney
Matt Roberts, Board Member (absent)
Kevin Dunkel, Board Member (absent)
Scott Giampaolo, Board Member (absent)
Doug Hudelson, Board Member

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and the east outside entryway
of the Miami Regional Airport Terminal and by posting on www.miamiokla.net starting at 9:45 PM on May 08, 2026,
pursuant to 25 O.S §311(9) (a) and (b).

THE BOARD MAY TAKE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING ACTIONS: DISCUSS, CONSIDER AND VOTE FOR APPROVAL,
ACCEPTANCE, REJECTION, AMENDMENT AND/OR POSTPONEMENT OF ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

1. Call to Order
Chairperson Jurgensmeyer called the meeting to order at 4:10pm.

2. Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances
None.

3. *Action Item* Minutes: February 09, 2026
Stafford made a motion to accept the minutes as presented. Stewart made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stafford, aye Stewart, aye Hudelson, aye Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, aye Giampaolo, absent            Dunkel, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the motion carried.

4. Financial Report
The board reviewed and discussed the March and April financial reports included in their packet.

No action taken.

5. *Discussion and Possible Action* Recommend FY 26/27 Budget to Miami City Council
Jones and Fitzgibbon reviewed the proposed FY 26/27 budget with the board. It was agreed to keep the estimated fuel
revenues at a conservative level due to the taxiway relocation grant, in where construction is estimated to begin next
spring.

Stewart made a motion to approve recommending the proposed FY 26/27 budget, as presented, to the City council as
presented. Stafford made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stewart, aye Stafford, aye Hudelson, aye Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, aye Giampaolo, absent            Dunkel, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the motion carried.

6. *Discussion and Possible Action* Recommend Tiered Discount Rate in Lieu of Flowage Fee
Jones and the board agreed that the long-term goal is to maintain a good relationship with Midland Flyers, who
currently have the authorization to bring fuel onsite and submit a $.25/gallon flowage fee. It was discussed that, in


      Airport Advisory Board                          Page 1 of 2                                   May 11, 2026
place of the flowage fee authorization, offering them a flat discount of $1.00 over the airport’s cost. They also directed
Jones to include in that discussion Midland’s current usage of airport property that is not included in the land lease.

No action taken.

7. Airport Report
Fitzgibbon advised the board of the following:
    1. Bill Tritthart of the DAV contacted the airport to arrange to use the new terminal for a Dream Flight event,
        where they take veterans on a flight in a wooden biplane.
    2. A fuel charge account process is being worked on.
    3. Hangars 9N and 3S will be open on 07/01/2026.
    4. Advised that 2 stop signs, along with an “Active Airfield” sign just beneath, will be installed.

9. Airport Board Future Planning
The board asked Jones to enforce the agricultural lease that requires the area to be cut at least two times per year.

10. Other New Business
None

11.*Action Item* Adjournment
Stewart made a motion to adjourn. Hudelson made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stewart, aye Hudelson, aye Stafford, aye Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, aye Giampaolo, absent               Dunkel, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the meeting adjourned at 5:08pm.
