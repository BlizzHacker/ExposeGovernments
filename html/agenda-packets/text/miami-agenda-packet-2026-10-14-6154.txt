   MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE MIAMI

                                      SENIORS’ CENTER, SEPTEMBER 9, 2026


The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and the front
door of The Miami Seniors’ Center and by posting on www.miamiok.gov pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311
(9)(a)(b).

1. On September 9, 2026 the Miami Seniors’ Center Board of Directors met for their monthly
meeting. The meeting was called to order at 10:02 am by President Mary Susan Whaley with the
following members answering present at roll call. There is one vacant position.

          Kevin Browning, Member
          Mark McDowell, Secretary
          Mary Susan Whaley, President
          Kathy Wright, Treasurer
          Caitlyn Baker, Center Director

2. There were no public comments.

3. *Action Item* Approval of August meeting minutes. After a discussion of the minutes,
Director Wright made a motion to approve the minutes with a second by Director Browning.
President Whaley called for a vote:

          Kevin Browning, Aye           Mark McDowell, Aye Mary Susan Whaley, Aye Kathy Wright, Aye

          President Whaley declared motion passed.

4. Director Caitlyn Baker presented the attached Senior Center report. Three additional assistants
will be hired to work at both the library and the Senior Center. She stated that the Smash and Dash
food truck will be available during the pool tournament, the September dance has been canceled,
and a membership drive may be held in January. Nominations for the vacant position were also
discussed. Kathy Wright asked if future reports could include information about facility rentals. The
group also considered holding a Mahjong tournament.

5. *Action Item* The 2027 meeting schedule was presented. It was approved upon a motion by
Kevin Browning followed by a second by Kathy Wright President Whaley called for a vote:




Miami Seniors’ Center, Inc   1 of 2                                                      Sept 2026
          Kevin Browning, Aye    Mark McDowell, Aye Mary Susan Whaley, Aye Kathy Wright, Aye

          President Whaley declared motion passed.

6. There was no action taken for future planning.

7. There was no other new business.

8. *Action Item* With no further business, Kathy Wright moved to adjourn, and Kevin Browning
seconded the motion. President Whaley adjourned the meeting at 10:25 AM.
