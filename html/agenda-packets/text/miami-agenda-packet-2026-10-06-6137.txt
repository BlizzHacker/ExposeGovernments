              SEWER CONNECTION GENERAL NOTES

      1.   ALL SEWER TAPS ARE THE RESPONSIBILITY OF THE PROPERTY OWNER AND MUST BE
           INSTALLED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE MIAMI SPECIAL UTILITY AUTHORITY'S (MSUA'S)
           STANDARDS.

      2.   WHEN REPLACING EXISTING OR INSTALLING A NEW SEWER TAP, THE OWNER WILL BE
           RESPONSIBLE FOR INSTALLING THE NEW TAP, INCLUDING THE SECTION OF SEWER MAIN
           NEEDED TO INSTALL THE TAP, WITH A PVC SEWER TEE OR PVC SEWER WYE. THE TAP MUST BE
           INSTALLED AT APPROXIMATELY 45 DEGREE ANGLE TO THE SEWER MAIN. IF A WYE IS USED IT
           MUST BE ORIENTED SO THAT THE FLOW IS IN THE CORRECT DIRECTION.

      3.   NO SEWER TAPS WILL BE ALLOWED TO COME IN THE TOP OF THE MAIN OR INTO ANY MANHOLE.

      4.   ALL SEWER TAPS SHALL HAVE A MINIMUM OF ONE 4 INCH PVC RISER WITH CAP EXTENDING 18
           INCHES ABOVE GRADE ON ALL CLEAN OUTS.

      5.   TAPS ON PVC SEWER MAIN, THE TAP SHALL BE INSTALLED BY REMOVING A SMALL SECTION OF
           THE SEWER MAIN AND TAP SHALL BE INSTALLED BY USING KNOCK ON PVC COUPLERS AND/OR
           THE FEMALE END OF THE TEE OR WYE.

      6.   TAPS ON CURED IN PLACE PIPE (CIPP) SEWER MAIN, THE TAP SHALL BE INSTALLED BY
           REMOVING A SECTION OF THE SEWER MAIN HOST PIPE AND INSTALLING A PVC SADDLE TEE
           AND STAINLESS STEEL BANDS. THE USE OF THE MSUA APPROVED SEWER CONNECTION SHALL
           BE USED TO CONNECT NEW PVC TAP TO EXISTING CIPP.

      7.   TAPS ON VITRIFIED CLAY OR CONCRETE SEWER MAIN, THE TAP SHALL BE INSTALLED BY
           REMOVING A SMALL SECTION OF THE SEWER MAIN. THE USE OF THE MSUA APPROVED SEWER
           CONNECTION SHALL BE USED TO CONNECT NEW PVC TAP TO EXISTING SEWER MAIN.

      8.   ALL SEWER TAPS MUST HAVE A MINIMUM OF 3 FEET OF SEPARATION.


      9.   ALL SANITARY SEWER LINES MUST HAVE A MINIMUM 2 FOOT SEPARATION VERTICALLY AND 10
           FEET HORIZONTALLY OR HAVE AN ENCASEMENT THAT EXTENDS 10 FEET EACH SIDE OF THE
           WATER LINE.

      10. ALL SEWER TAPS MUST BE INSPECTED BY A REPRESENTATIVE OF MSUA SEWER DEPARTMENT.
          PICTURES, AND GPS DATA WILL BE GATHERED BEFORE BACKFILLING WILL BE ALLOWED.

      11. ROAD AND ALLEY CUTS WILL REQUIRE A PERMIT FROM THE CITY'S STREET DEPARTMENT.
          CONCRETE ROAD AND ALLEY CUTS WILL BE REQUIRED TO BE PATCHED A MINIMUM OF 1 FOOT
          EACH SIDE OF THE DITCH WITH A MINIMUM OF 6 INCHES IN THICKNESS ON CONCRETE ROADS
          AND WILL BE REQUIRED TO REPLACE FORM SEAM TO SEAM ON AND FROM CENTERLINE TO
          EDGE OF THE ROAD. ASPHALT ROAD AND ALLEY CUTS WILL BE REQUIRED TO BE PATCHED A
          MINIMUM OF 2 FEET EACH SIDE OF THE DITCH WITH A MINIMUM OF 6 INCHES IN THICKNESS AND
          A 2 INCH MINIMUM ASPHALT ON TOP TO COMPLETE THE ROAD CUT REPAIR.

      12. SEWER LINE THAT CROSSES AN EXISTING STREET WILL BE REQUIRED TO BE SLEEVED UNDER
          THE ROAD. THE SLEEVE WILL BE A MINIMUM OF SCHEDULE 40 PVC AND EXTEND 2 FEET PAST
          THE EDGE OF THE ROAD.

      13. DITCHES WILL BE BEDDED WITH 4 INCH MINIMUM OF AGGREGATE BEDDING AND BACK FILL WITH
          A MINIMUM 6 INCH EACH SIDE AND 12 INCH AGGREGATE COVER. ROAD AND ALLEY CUT WILL BE
          BACKFILLED WITH AGGREGATE UP TO POINT OF CONCRETE OR TOP OF DITCH IF NOT
          CONCRETE OR ASPHALT. AGGREGATE SHALL BE SMALLER THAN 1.25 INCHES IN DIAMETER.
