Main Street Coordinator Report:
Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Main Street will start hosting a food truck night once
a month. We are calling it After Hours on Main. Looking at the second Thursday of the month. The end
of the month is Heritage Fest. All of the CVB team will be working during the event. Some upcoming
events in October and November are Sweet Street and Holiday Market. Board member Jones brought up
making facebook events for each event. Board member Frazier asked about having fliers for events, such
as Holiday Market fliers in September.
