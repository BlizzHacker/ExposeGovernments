            MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
                    OF THE MIAMI SENIORS’ CENTER, JUNE 10, 2026

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of The Miami Civic Center and on the front door
of The Miami Seniors’ Center and by posting on www.miamiokla.net pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) (b).

    1. On June 10th, 2026, The Miami Seniors Center Board of Directors met for their
       monthly meeting. The meeting was called to order at 10:00 am, by President Mary
       Susan Whaley with the following members answering present at roll call.

                   Kevin Browning, Member
                   Mary Susan Whaley, President
                   Kathy Wright, Treasurer
                   Callie Cortner, MSC Director

                   Absent: Vacant Position
                           Mark McDowell

    2. Public Input or Appearances- None

    3. *Action Item* Approval of April Minutes After discussion of the minutes, Director
       Wright made a motion to approve the minutes as read. Director Browning a second
       to the motion. President Whaley called for a vote:

         Browning, Aye McDowell, Absent            Whaley, Aye       Wright, Aye       VACANCY

                   President Whaley declared motion carried.

    4. *Action Item* Approval of Annual Meeting (May) Minutes After discussion of the
        minutes, Director Wright made a motion to approve the minutes as read. Director
        Browning a second to the motion. President Whaley called for a vote:

         Browning, Aye McDowell, Absent            Whaley, Aye       Wright, Aye       VACANCY

                   President Whaley declared motion carried


     5. *Action Item* DOCS Services The discussion was tabled about whether the
         Senior Center staff would continue scheduling and buying the purchase of
         DOCS meals. Wright made the motion to table, with a second by Browning.

     6. A monthly Senior Center report was presented by Callie Cortner. Callie will look
     into working with OCI to adapt the Multi-Purpose Room to better serve the needs of the
     center. She also mentioned that several agencies are renting the facility for their
     events and that is an additional source of income. A financial report was also given.

     7. There was no other business that needed to be discussed

     8. *Action Item* Adjournment There being no further business Director Wright made
         a motion to adjourn with a second by Director Browning. President Whaley called
         for a vote:

          Browning, Aye McDowell, Absent Whaley, Aye Wright, Aye VACANCY

                    President Whaley declared motion carried and adjourned at 10:28 AM
