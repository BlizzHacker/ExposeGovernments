THE AIRPORT ADVISORY BOARD MET IN REGULAR SESSION ON AUGUST 10, 2026, IN THE MIAMI REGIONAL AIRPORT
TERMINAL BUILDING AT 4:00PM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Pat Jurgensmeyer, Chairperson                                         Travis Jones, Airport Director
Jeff Stewart, Board Member (absent)                                   Jill Fitzgibbon, Airport Ofc Coordinator (absent)
Kyle Stafford, Board Member                                           Molly Olson, Deputy City Clerk
Matt Roberts, Board Member (absent)
Kevin Dunkel, Board Member
Scott Giampaolo, Board Member (absent)

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and the east outside entryway
of the Miami Regional Airport Terminal and by posting on www.miamiok.gov starting at 10:45 AM on August 05, 2026,
pursuant to 25 O.S §311(9) (a) and (b).

THE BOARD MAY TAKE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING ACTIONS: DISCUSS, CONSIDER AND VOTE FOR APPROVAL,
ACCEPTANCE, REJECTION, AMENDMENT AND/OR POSTPONEMENT OF ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

1. Call to Order
Chairperson Jurgensmeyer called the meeting to order at 4:13pm.

2. Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances
None.

3. *Action Item* Minutes: May 11, 2026
Stafford made a motion to accept the minutes as presented. Hudelson made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stafford, aye Hudelson, aye Dunkel, abstain Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, absent Giampaolo, absent Stewart, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the motion carried.

4. Financial Report
The board reviewed and discussed the June and July financial reports included in their packet. Jones let the Board know
that money was still coming in for June 2026, so the financial report numbers for Fiscal Year 2025-2026 are not final.

No action taken.

5. *Discussion* Using Taxiway Millings for Airport Parking Lot
Jones explained that using taxiway relocation project millings to complete the Airport parking lot will result in a
potential four-hundred-thousand-dollar ($400,000.00) savings. He also reported that the project, paid for with FAA
and State grant monies along with the City’s match, came in two million dollars ($2,000,000.00) over the budgeted
amount.

No action taken.

6. *Discussion* Drainage Plan for the Airport Property
Jones explained that the Airport engineers from Garver looked at the area north of the hangars and recommended
taking out the elevated portion of the ground and digging into the existing ditch to make it slightly larger. The City will
do the work, and there is no anticipated expense besides City equipment and labor.

No action taken.

7. *Discussion and Possible Action* to Move the October 12, 2026, Airport Board Meeting
The board agreed to cancel the meeting on October 12, 2026, and reschedule a special meeting on October 19, 2026,
if necessary.

Stafford made a motion to cancel the meeting on October 12, 2026, and reschedule a special meeting for October 19,
2026, if necessary. Dunkel made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stafford, aye Dunkel, aye Hudelson, aye Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, absent Giampaolo, absent            Stewart, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the motion carried.

8. Airport Report
Jones notified the Board of the following:
    1. $52,500 will be moved from the budgeted capital improvement carryover to cover the increase to the City’s
        match on the taxiway relocation project, where the bids came in $2M over budget.
    2. New chair rail was installed in the conference room in the terminal building.
    3. The City Council approved the Airport Fee Schedule, which now includes a fifty-five-dollar ($55.00) call-out fee
        for after-hours call-outs.
    4. Bids have been received, and work will begin to raise the fuel basin to keep us compliant with the City’s Spill
        Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure Plan.

No action taken.

9. Airport Board Future Planning
Chairperson Jurgensmeyer invited Travis Reisch to share with the Board. Reisch shared the following:
    1. The $10,000 agricultural contract is not good for pilots coming in and going out, and he’d rather see the City
        put in a natural grass runway.
    2. The Old Terminal Building should be rented out to someone to generate additional revenue.
    3. The Board could potentially attract a teacher if they provided airplanes for training.
    4. The fence should be finished to secure the Airport.

Stafford notified the Board of grants that are available to build new hangars.

10. Other New Business
None.

11.*Action Item* Adjournment
Stafford made a motion to adjourn. Dunkel made the second. The motion carried by vote:

Stafford, aye Dunkel, aye Hudelson, aye Jurgensmeyer, aye Roberts, absent Giampaolo, absent            Stewart, absent

Chairperson Jurgensmeyer declared the meeting adjourned at 5:04pm.
