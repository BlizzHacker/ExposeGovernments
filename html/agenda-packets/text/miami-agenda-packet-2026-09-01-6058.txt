THE TRUST MAY DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE ON ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

Call to Order
Board President Crow called the meeting to order at 8:34 A.M

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances
None

Discuss and Approve July 2026 Meeting Minutes:
Board Member Jones motioned to approve the July Minutes. Board Member Frazier made the second.
The board was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye, Chenoweth: Absent, Frisbie: Absent

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Main Street Coordinator Report:
Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. The first After Hours on Main had a good turnout.
The next is scheduled for August 13. Heritage fest is over now, and the next big event is in September for
Junktique on September 19. After that we will have our October Sip and Shop on the 27 th and Sweet
Street on October 30th.

None
Discussion and Possible Action on Department of Commerce Suggested Temporary 4 Point Committee
Restructuring:
Trinda gave a report on the 4 point committees. Trinda and Jesse had an impromptu meeting with Tamara
Nelson, as well as Karla Jackson and Anna Schilling from Oklahoma Main Street while they were in the
area. The meeting was primarily about the 501-C3 progress. They also discussed the committees, and
suggested we focus on Organization Committee, especially with getting volunteers, potential committee
members and telling Miami Main Street’s story. The board discussed having a volunteer card that people
could get to the CVB Office and Jesse. Attending Intertribal Council, and putting the word out about asking
for volunteers.

None
Update and Discussion on 501C3:
Trinda Crow began discussion on the 501C3. After calling the IRS, we do not have an existing 501-C3.
However, we believe the EZ form can be done for us. We do not have a timeline, but Crow spoke on Fire
Ball’s 501-C3 and it was around 6 weeks for that to get done.

Other New Business, if any, which has Arisen since the Posting of the Agenda and Could
Not Have been Anticipated Prior to the Time of Posting (25 O.S. §311 (9))
Debbie East asked to double check on music for Holiday Market. Do we want a Piano again in the lobby.
We liked having it last year.

Adjournment
Board Member Jones motioned to adjourn. Board Member Frazier made the second. The board was
polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye, Chenoweth: Absent, Frisbie: Absent

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Adjourned at 9:37
