explained that the electric meters will begin arriving in January/February 2027 and will be installed by

implementation will begin. Lollar explained that the materials for fiber show up daily. Construction for the
outside fiber plant has begun and there will be an access gate installed. There are two more bids that will
be opened for the material and labor of the drops. Crews will begin hanging fiber after the new year.
