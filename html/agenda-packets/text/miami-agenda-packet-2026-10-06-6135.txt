Board Member Jones motioned to approve the August Minutes. Board Member Frazier made the second.

Main Street Coordinator Report:
Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. The next After Hours on Main is coming up
September 10. There will be karaoke. 66 Junktique Market is September 19th. After that, Sweet Street
and Holiday Market will round out the year for us. The city has requested Main Street take over
purchasing the flowers on Main.

None
Update on 501C3 Status:
Trinda Crow began discussion on the 501C3. She met with the interim attorney for the city. Trinda
brought examples of other Main Street program bylaws. They are being worked on. Trying the easy route.

2027 Event Calendar Planning
The Main Street Board began planning the 2027 calendar for Main Street. Jesse had put in dates for events
the city had already announced, and had After Hours on Main dates. They will pick back up in April after
December. Also looking to combine some sip and shops with it to make a shopping day. Discussion was
made to move the dinner with moving to Thursday, the dinner could be a different time and allow for it
to not be the same week as the CAC dinner which has moved back to June. Jesse will take the suggested
dates and rewrite the calendar to be voted on in October.
