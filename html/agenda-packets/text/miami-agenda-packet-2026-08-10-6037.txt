Miami Main Street Report:
Jesse Black gave an update on Main Street. Starting July 16, Main Street will be starting a monthly food
truck night. It will be called “After Hours on Main” and will be once a month. Future dates are August 13,
September 10, October 8, November 13 with the Holiday Market, and December 17 with a Sip and Shop.
66 Junktique Market is in September and have had several calls to reserve spots since posting the info
again. Looking ahead Sweet Street will be October 30, and the Holiday Market November 13 and 14.

none

Convention And Visitors Bureau Office Report:
Trinda gave the report on the CVB. The CVB team has Heritage Fest. The festival is pretty much ready to
go, just have some fine tuning to take care of. Rigs will come in Friday morning, followed shortly by
vendors. There are scheduled interviews with Good Morning Four states, KKOW radio interviews, and
Living Well. On September 12, Miami, Commerce, and Quapaw will have the Tri-City Cruise Night. The
CVB is starting a new marketing strategy, wanting to keep momentum after the centennial.

None
Hotel/Motel Tax Report:
Lauren Hart gave a review of the CVB hotel tax. June’s hotel taxes are not in yet, but it is ahead 4%. That
is without June which had 8-Man Football.

No action was taken

Heritage Fest Update:
Trinda Crow gave some updates on Heritage Fest. There has been a huge response on Facebook. There
is plenty of marketing out there. Chris Lane did a promo video for the festival with over 13,000 views.
Jesse brought up that Main Street businesses are encouraged to stay open and let visitors know on social
media that their back entrances are open.

none
Grant Updates:

was from the state has been put up as well. Crow is in process of working on the obelisk. The project is
almost ready to go out to get bids.
