Miami Main Street Report:
Jesse Black gave a report on Main Street. Main Street events are picking back up after assisting with
Heritage Fest. September 19 will be the first 66 Junktique Market and spots are beginning to fill up. Sweet
Street is on October 30 and Holiday Market will be on November 13-14.

none
Convention And Visitors Bureau Office Report:
Trinda gave the report on the CVB. Heritage Fest was a success, and more will be covered in the 2026
Heritage Fest Update. Moving on to the Rodeo now

None
Heritage Fest Update:
Trinda Crow gave some updates on Heritage Fest. It was a good event. The main issue was the heat, but
it was successful. Placer showed around 10,000 people, and there was no accidents reported to CVB staff.
Currently the Heritage Fest committee is looking to move the festival to the fall.

none
Upcoming Fall Events:
Trinda gave a list of upcoming events. Between the CVB and the Centennial, some upcoming events are
the Rodeo, The Tri City Cruise Night, an Airstream Rally on Main, the Route 66 Birthday Bash, and the New
Years Eve Ball Drop.
