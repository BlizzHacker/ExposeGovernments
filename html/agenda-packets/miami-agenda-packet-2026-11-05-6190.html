THE CITY ZONING AND PLANNING COMMISSION MET IN SPECIAL SESSION ON TUESDAY, JULY 14, 2026, IN THE

Notice of special meeting of the City Zoning and Planning Commission, called by Chairperson Tim Ingram was

25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) and (b) and the agenda was posted in the same manner at 4:15 PM on July 09, 2026.


THE COMMISSION MAY TAKE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING ACTIONS: DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE FOR
APPROVAL, ACCEPTANCE, REJECTION, AMENDMENT AND OR POSTPONEMENT OF ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS
AGENDA:

1. Call to Order
Chairperson Ingram called the City Zoning and Planning Commission meeting to order at 4:01PM.

2. Approve Minutes: April 30, 2026 (Special Meeting)
No action was taken.

3. Public Hearing Concerning the Rezoning of the Below Properties to RM-1 Residential Multifamily
Property Address: 1834 A NE
Legal Address: Lots 5, 6, & 7, Block 2, Castle Park Addition to the City of Miami,
Ottawa County, Oklahoma, according to the recorded Plat thereof.

Chairperson Ingram opened the public hearing at 4:01PM

Chris Randolph explained that Par H Homes is requesting the zoning change to residential multifamily so that
the owner can renovate the old existing church into apartments. The plan is to start with four (4) two (2)
bedroom one (1) bathroom apartments and then phase two (2) will be to add additional units. All of the
construction will be contained within the current building. The map included in the packet is the original plat
map with the original street names, which have changed overtime. The number of current parking spaces is
adequate for the first phase. There will be additional parking behind the apartments on B NE.

Chuck Chesnut explained that the current building is empty and the community needs housing. Chesnut stated
that the owner is providing a benefit to the city by adding much needed housing.

Rachel Buhl requested the planning commission members reject the proposal. Buhl explained that she believes
that there needs to be studies conducted to determine the impact on the current infrastructure. Buhl explained
that the street is narrow and she is concerned about the extra amount of traffic.

Chris Randolph explained that Rachel Buhl submitted questions and those questions have been sent to the
contractor and engineer for a response. Randoph explained that he is not aware of the church being a historical
landmark.

5. Public Hearing Concerning the Approval of a Commercial Business Within the Commercial Park, Currently
Zoned as Planned Unit Development (PUD)
Chairperson Ingram opened the public hearing at 4:22PM

Chris Randolph explained that the request is to approve of a commercial business within the planned unit
development located in the commercial park. The property is just south of the Sinclair gas station located on N.
Elm Street. Dollar General has purchased the property and because the property is a planned unit development
they must seek approval from the zoning and planning commission.

Chairperson Ingram closed the public hearing at 4:25PM

6. Discuss, Consider, and Vote on the Approval of a Commercial Business Within the Commercial Park,
Currently Zoned as Planned Unit Development (PUD)
Commission Member Thompson made a motion to approve the commercial business within the commercial
park currently zoned as a planned unit development (PUD). Commission Member Shirley seconded the motion.

Chairperson Ingram declared the motion carried.
