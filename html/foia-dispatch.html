#4 — City Social Media Records (All Platforms)

Agency: City Clerk, City of Miami, OK | Email: cityclerk@miamiok.gov
Pursuant to the Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. § 24A.1 et seq.), the Move Weight Foundation, a 501(c)(3) transparency organization, respectfully requests complete records of the City of Miami's social media presence across all platforms. Specifically, we request the complete content, metadata, and activity records for every social media account operated by or on behalf of the City of Miami, its departments, elected officials, and employees acting in their official capacity. This includes but is not limited to: 1. FACEBOOK: - The City's official page (facebook.com/miamiokla) and any departmental pages - All posts, comments, reactions, shares, messages, and page management activity - All hidden, deleted, or restricted-visibility content with timestamps and author information - Complete administrator and moderator lists with access levels - Facebook provides a one-click "Download Your Information" tool that exports all page data as a structured ZIP file: facebook.com/help/212802592074644 2. OTHER PLATFORMS: - Any Twitter/X, Instagram, YouTube, Nextdoor, or other social media accounts - Equivalent complete records for each platform 3. SOCIAL MEDIA POLICIES: - The City's current social media policy, content moderation guidelines, and records retention policy - Any training materials provided to employees regarding social media use - Any records of content removal decisions, including who authorized removal and the reason In accordance with 51 O.S. § 24A.5, the Foundation is prepared to pay reasonable fees. To minimize burden on City staff, we note that most social media platforms provide automated data export functions. If any portion is denied, please identify each record withheld, the specific statutory exemption, and the authorizing individual per 51 O.S. § 24A.7. Submitted by Move Weight Foundation, a 501(c)(3) Transparency Organization.
Send via FOIA Form
