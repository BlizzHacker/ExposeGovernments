Coleman Storefront Windows
Gretchen Frazier, Owner of Flint Creek Bath made an appeal to the board concerning the front windows
of the Coleman Shops. She understands that repairs are needing to happen. She requests the tile
underneath be repaired and every step taken to keep the aluminum trim of the windows to keep the
stores matching the look of the Coleman Theatre.
none
Building Repair Project:
The Chairman immediately gave the floor to Kevin Browning to speak on the Coleman Building repair. He
doesn’t disagree with keeping the look. The tile is breaking due to weathering and rot and definitely needs
to be replaced. There is a bid to replace the windows and go with a thicker pane that would not be as
easy to break. He wants to be sure the board is thinking what will happen if there is damage. For many
years there were many band aids put on issues at the Coleman and there are many projects that will need
to be updated, such as the fire escape, the boilers, heat and Air.. Some current cost estimates would be
$34,000 to rebuild the bottom with the tile. Another $34,000 for the doors and windows, and finally $9000
for the upper windows. Some other projects that need to be done are the boilers, Central Heat and Air
being replaced next week. The city has a major problem with the fire escape in its current state.

Gretchen had a contact that could do the windows and keep the trim. The new goals are to get a quote
on the fire escape. Get numbers to bring next month. The concerns are the liability from fragile windows,
and the money from MDRA being used effectively for the city building. There are currently three bids for
the windows from Oklahoma Glass, Joplin Glass, and Draft Brothers. Other Coleman Shop owners were
asked their opinion. Carole from the Friends of the Coleman hopes the copper can be kept. Jason
remembered a cap made to help with missing copper. Suggests that chatgpt could help with finding tile
businesses. Wants the new windows to last, but anything that can be saved would be preferred.

None
Coleman Theatre Managing Director Report
Danny Dillon gave the Coleman Director’s report. The winter is the slower season, but the Coleman has
lots of tours upcoming. There are shows upcoming including a comedy show January 22, The Whitney
Houston Experience January 31, and an illusionist wanting to do 50 states in 50 days wants to come to the
Coleman. A Selena tribute on February 20th. Lots of shows upcoming.

None
Main Street Update:
Jesse Black gave an update on Main Street. The New Years Eve Ball Drop was held for the first time and
was a huge success. PlacerAi registered 848 people and that only counts cell phones, so the numbers are

Display. Those were requested by the Main Street Committees. There is a Sip and Shop coming up on
February 10, and a new shopping event, Where’s Bless O’Parker the week of St. Patrick’s Day.

CVB and Tourism Update February 2026

Things have been busy in the CVB office. Here is a recap of activities:

• The details are being formed for the Oklahoma Mayors Summer Conference in June.
Bless and I have met with the conference coordinator and plans are moving forward.
• Heritage Fest planning began in the fall of 2025. We are confirming bands,
contracts, food vendors, vendors, plans with Rigs on Route 66, sponsorship packets
and other details. Details will be announced soon. Heritage Fest July 24 & 25, 2026.
• The grant for the Obelisk has been signed and set back to the city. We will begin the
process of collecting bids for the work and move forward, when appropriate, with
the construction.
• Still working on the Wayfinding signs with Colby Allen.
• I met with the design team for the large Route 66 sign that will be given to each city
on the route from the Department of Commerce. Miami’s design will be sent to us in
several months for design approval. Allen Signs is making all the Route 66 designs
for this project. These signs are 6’ X 6’.
• I’ve been working with Melinda Stotts, Barry Linduff and John Rogers on a save-the-
date video for Centennial events. This video has been completed and is posted on
the Visit Miami and Miami Main Street Facebook pages.
• Jesse and I have been working together on several projects: Main 66 Venue, National
Convention mobile workshop in April, yearly partnerships with businesses and
some MMS events planned for this fall.
• Sunday February 8th, I attended the Oklahoma Route 66 Association Annual Board
meeting as well as the regular quarterly meeting in Oklahoma City. Both these
meetings updated on state events along the route as well as legislation and fact-
finding topic updates.
• I attended the American Bus Association Marketplace in January in Reno, Nevada. I
spoke with 64 bus tour businesses and promoted Miami and the Coleman Theatre.
• Our visitors traveling along the route are beginning to pick up and seem excited to be
learning about the route during its Centennial year.

Please call or stop by anytime with questions and just for a visit!

Building Maintenance Update and Possible Vote on Storefront Window Project:

Kevin Browning presented an update on the building. For the windows Browning gave a quote on cost of
repairing as is, or building brand new. The differences being quoted $36,000, or $53,500 from two
different quotes for as is, as opposed to $77,000 for building new. The benefits of repairing as is would
allow more funds to be used elsewhere on repairs in the Coleman. The benefits of brand new would be
knowing better what we get and sealing everything up. There are some contractors that could do it as is.
They believe they can do it. Either way it would need to have a quick timeframe to get each window and
not disrupt business. The windows have leaked water before, and it could again. Tempered glass would
take at least a couple of weeks to get here. There would be a warranty for either. The main goal would
be making it last. Trustee Morgan asked about other projects that could use the extra funds if the trust
went with going as is. Browning mentioned the fire escape as brought up last meeting would need
$48,000 to $52,500. There is always a place where the money can go. The trust discussed the benefit of
being able to tackle more projects.

Trustee Estep moved to approve fixing the window as is instead of brand new. Trustee Stotts the
second. The trust was polled with the following results:
