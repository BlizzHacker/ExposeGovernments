Main Street Coordinator Report:
Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Black thanked the promotion committee for the
suggestion of the workshops. There is a Sip & Shop on February 10. The Main Street National Conference
is April 12-16. Jesse and Trinda will be attending and Miami is hosting a Mobile Workshop. Black gave
details for the Main Street Dinner. It is scheduled for June 5th. Organization committee has been asked
to lead collecting donations and Design committee has been asked for assistance in decorating. The Civic
Center has been booked in case of bad weather as a backup. Black then opened discussion about
Junktique. Asking for a name suggestion when advertising so people know what it is. A suggested booth
cost was discussed at 20 dollars. The name “66 Junktique Market” was decided on. Jesse had Trinda Crow
share news about the former Main Attraction building. All city departments were challenged with finding
a source of funding. The former Main Attraction Building is now going to be “Main 66 Venue” It is an
event center and Main Street is in charge of booking and keeping it clean, but will receive funds from
events and share with MSUA who owns and maintains the building.

Discussion Regarding Logistics “Where’s Bless O’Parker” Week
Jesse Black then began discussion about the week of St. Patricks Day. He wanted input from Board
Members especially business owners. The idea is to have a cutout of Mayor Parker as a leprechaun. It
would be hid in stores with businesses offering a discount to those who find it. Then it would be re-hidden
later. Mayor Parker would also visit stores dressed up as “Bless O’Parker” and Main Street is asking
businesses to hold some sort of flash sale while he is there. Business owners were asked if Jesse and
Trinda should do the hiding or would they rather hide to showcase certain areas of the store. Main Street
does not want to inconvenience the businesses, but wants to have a different type of event. Board
members in attendance suggested the business hiding themselves to focus on areas, and also to be able
to space out hiding times.
