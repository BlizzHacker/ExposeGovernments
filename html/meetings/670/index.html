Looking ahead the Dinner will be in June and then we will be in summer with the Mayor’s Conference, 8-man, and Heritage Fest coming up.

None 4 Point Committee Chair Reports: The committee chairs gave an update on the 4 Point Committees. Gretchen Frazier gave an update on Design. Center Pieces are being acquired. Debbie is working on a Mariachi Band for Sip and Shop. Design will be planting flowers on May 6th at 4:00 Jason had no report on Promotion Committee. Tori Chenoweth gave an update on The Economic Vitality Committee. Business packets are done. Organization is working on donations for the auction. Working on the seniors banners. None Main 66 Venue Update Trinda Crow gave a report on the Main 66 Venue. Word is out about the venue and there have been many bookings and calls. We plan to have its own facebook to make communication easier about the venue.

none Other New Business, if any, which has Arisen since the Posting of the Agenda and Could Not Have been Anticipated Prior to the Time of Posting (25 O.S. §311 (9)) The Obelisk is being designed from the Route 66 Grant.
