📄 Official Minutes

THE MIAMI MAIN STREET BOARD MET IN REGULAR MEETING TUESDAY FEBRUARY 3, 2026 IN THE MIAMI CONVENTION & VISITORS BUREAU AT 8:32 AM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Trinda Crow, Board President

Gretchen Frazier, Board Member

Tori Chenoweth, Board Member

Kyla Jones, Board Member

Sonja Frisbie, Board Member (Absent)

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and by posting on www.miamiokla.net starting at 4:00PM on January 28, 2026, pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) and (b). THE TRUST MAY DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE ON ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

Board President Crow called the meeting to order at 8:32 A.M

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances

Discuss and Approve November Meeting Minutes:

Board Member Jones motioned to approve the December Minutes. Board Member Frazier made the second. The board was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Chenoweth: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye, Frisbie: Absent

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Main Street Coordinator Report:

Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Black thanked the promotion committee for the suggestion of the workshops. There is a Sip & Shop on February 10. The Main Street National Conference is April 12-16. Jesse and Trinda will be attending and Miami is hosting a Mobile Workshop. Black gave details for the Main Street Dinner. It is scheduled for June 5". Organization committee has been asked to lead collecting donations and Design committee has been asked for assistance in decorating. The Civic Center has been booked in case of bad weather as a backup. Black then opened discussion about Junktique. Asking for a name suggestion when advertising so people know what it is. A suggested booth cost was discussed at 20 dollars. The name “66 Junktique Market” was decided on. Jesse had Trinda Crow share news about the former Main Attraction building. All city departments were challenged with finding a source of funding. The former Main Attraction Building is now going to be “Main 66 Venue” It is an event center and Main Street is in charge of booking and keeping it clean, but will receive funds from events and share with MSUA who owns and maintains the building.

4 Point Committee Chair Reports:

The committee chairs gave an update on the 4 Point Committees. Gretchen Frazier and Jason Miller did not have any new report from Design or Promotion respectively.

Tori Chenoweth gave an update on The Economic Vitality Committee. Chenoweth and Jesse met with Cindy Morris of the Chamber of Commerce and Shannon Duhon of Miami Area Economic Development in December to get ideas and collaborate on having downtown surveys Sonja Frisbie was absent and there was no report on Organization committee. None ~ ~ Discussion Regarding Logistics “Where’s Bless O’Parker” Week Jesse Black then began discussion about the week of St. Patricks Day. He wanted input from Board Members especially business owners. The idea is to have a cutout of Mayor Parker as a leprechaun. It would be hid in stores with businesses offering a discount to those who find it. Then it would be re-hidden later. Mayor Parker would also visit stores dressed up as “Bless O’Parker” and Main Street is asking businesses to hold some sort of flash sale while he is there. Business owners were asked if Jesse and Trinda should do the hiding or would they rather hide to showcase certain areas of the store. Main Street does not want to inconvenience the businesses, but wants to have a different type of event. Board members in attendance suggested the business hiding themselves to focus on areas, and also to be able to space out hiding times.
