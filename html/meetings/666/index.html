THE MIAMI MAIN STREET BOARD MET IN REGULAR MEETING TUESDAY MARCH 3, 2026 IN THE MIAMI CONVENTION & VISITORS BUREAU AT 8:3 AM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Trinda Crow, Board President Gretchen Frazier, Board Member Tori Chenoweth, Board Member Kyla Jones, Board Member Sonja Frisbie, Board Member

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and by posting on www.miamiokla.net starting at 10:20AM on February 25, 2026, pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) and (b).

THE TRUST MAY DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE ON ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:

Call to Order Board President Crow called the meeting to order at 8:3 A.M

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances None

Discuss and Approve February Meeting Minutes: Board Member Jones motioned to approve the February Minutes. Board Member Frazier made the second. The board was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Chenoweth: Aye, Frisbie: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Main Street Coordinator Report: Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. March 16-20 will be “Where’s Bless O’Parker week. It will be a new event where shoppers can look for a laminated cutout of Bless to receive prizes or discounts with the Mayor visiting stores dressed as Bless O’Parker. Jesse and Trinda are attending the Main Street National Conference in Tulsa April 13-15. Looking forward to networking with Main Streeters from around the nation. The next event is the Senioritas Sip and Shop May 5th. Main 66 Venue is officially open after undergoing some renovations. The board was invited to see the finished look after the meeting

None 4 Point Committee Chair Reports: The committee chairs gave an update on the 4 Point Committees. Gretchen Frazier has been in discussion about the flowers for the pots on Main Street. Rotary is purchasing evergreen for the center of pots as part of an in kind with using Main 66 for weekly meetings. Jason was absent and there was no report on Promotion Committee. Tori Chenoweth gave an update on The Economic Vitality Committee. Business Flyers are ready to send. Sonja Frisbie is needing to resign as Organization Committee chair. She still wishes to remain on the committee. Looking for a committee chair. None Other New Business, if any, which has Arisen since the Posting of the Agenda and Could Not Have been Anticipated Prior to the Time of Posting (25 O.S. §311 (9)) None

Adjournment Board Member Jones motioned to adjourn. Board Member Frazier made the second. The board was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Chenoweth: Aye, Frisbie: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Adjourned at 9:07

Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Bless O’Parker seemed to be a good week. Next week Trinda and Jesse would be attending the Main Street National Conference in Tulsa and Miami’s Mobile Workshop would be the Sunday before. Following that, Trinda and Jesse would be attending Route 66 Day at the Capital April 21, and Main Street Day at the Capital on May 4°". The Senioritas Sip and Shop would be the following day. Looking ahead the Dinner will be in June and then we will be in summer with the Mayor’s Conference, 8-man, and Heritage Fest coming up.
