THE MIAMI MAIN STREET BOARD MET IN REGULAR MEETING TUESDAY APRIL 7, 2026 IN THE MIAMI
CONVENTION & VISITORS BUREAU AT 8:30 AM WITH THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS PRESENT:

Trinda Crow, Board President

Gretchen Frazier, Board Member

Tori Chenoweth, Board Member

Kyla Jones, Board Member

Sonja Frisbie, Board Member

The agenda for the meeting was displayed in the main lobby of the Miami Civic Center and by posting on
www.miamiokla.net starting at 9:00 AM on April 2, 2026, pursuant to 25 O.S. § 311(9) (a) and (b).

THE TRUST MAY DISCUSS, CONSIDER, AND VOTE ON ANY ITEM LISTED IN THIS AGENDA:


Board President Crow called the meeting to order at 8:30 A.M

Public Input and Unscheduled Personal Appearances


Discuss and Approve March 2026 Meeting Minutes:

Board President Crow motioned to approve the March Minutes. Board Member Jones made the second.
The board was polled with the following results:

Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Chenoweth: Aye, Frisbie: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye

Board President Crow declared the motion carried.

Main Street Coordinator Report:

Jesse Black gave the Main Street Coordinator report. Bless O’Parker seemed to be a good week. Next
week Trinda and Jesse would be attending the Main Street National Conference in Tulsa and Miami’s
Mobile Workshop would be the Sunday before. Following that, Trinda and Jesse would be attending
Route 66 Day at the Capital April 21, and Main Street Day at the Capital on May 4°". The Senioritas Sip
and Shop would be the following day. Looking ahead the Dinner will be in June and then we will be in
summer with the Mayor’s Conference, 8-man, and Heritage Fest coming up.


4 Point Committee Chair Reports:

The committee chairs gave an update on the 4 Point Committees.

Gretchen Frazier gave an update on Design. Center Pieces are being acquired. Debbie is working on a
Mariachi Band for Sip and Shop. Design will be planting flowers on May 6" at 4:00

Jason had no report on Promotion Committee.

Tori Chenoweth gave an update on The Economic Vitality Committee. Business packets are done.
Organization is working on donations for the auction. Working on the seniors banners.


Main 66 Venue Update
Trinda Crow gave a report on the Main 66 Venue. Word is out about the venue and there have been many
bookings and calls. We plan to have its own facebook to make communication easier about the venue.
Other New Business, if any, which has Arisen since the Posting of the Agenda and Could
Not Have been Anticipated Prior to the Time of Posting (25 O.S. §311 (9))
The Obelisk is being designed from the Route 66 Grant. The Dobson Museum is having its reopening this
week with Dobson Days on Saturday April 11.
Adjournment
Board Member Jones motioned to adjourn. Board Member Frazier made the second. The board was
polled with the following results:
Jones: Aye, Frazier: Aye, Chenoweth: Aye, Frisbie: Aye, Board President Crow: Aye
Board President Crow declared the motion carried.
Adjourned at 8:55
