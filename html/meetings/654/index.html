Coleman Storefront Windows

Gretchen Frazier, Owner of Flint Creek Bath made an appeal to the board concerning the front windows of the Coleman Shops. She understands that repairs are needing to happen. She requests the tile underneath be repaired and every step taken to keep the aluminum trim of the windows to keep the stores matching the look of the Coleman Theatre.

Building Repair Project:

The Chairman immediately gave the floor to Kevin Browning to speak on the Coleman Building repair. He doesn’t disagree with keeping the look. The tile is breaking due to weathering and rot and definitely needs to be replaced. There is a bid to repiace the windows and go with a thicker pane that would not be as easy to break. He wants to be sure the board is thinking what will happen if there is damage. For many years there were many band aics put on issues at the Coleman and there are many projects that will need to be updated, such as the fire escape, the boilers, heat and Air.. Some current cost estimates would be $34,000 to rebuild the bottom with the tile. Another $34,000 for the doors and windows, and finally $9000 for the upper windows. Some other projects that need to be done are the boilers, Central Heat and Air being replaced next week. The city has a major problem with the fire escape in its current state. Gretchen had a contact that could do the windows and keep the trim. The new goals are to get a quote on the fire escape. Get numbers to bring next month. The concerns are the liability from fragile windows, and the money from MDRA being used effectively for the city building. There are currently three bids for the windows from Oklahoma Glass, Joplin Glass, and Draft Brothers. Other Coleman Shop owners were asked their opinion. Carole from the Friends of the Coleman hopes the copper can be kept. Jason remembered a cap made to help with missing copper. Suggests that chatgpt could help with finding tile businesses. Wants the new windows to last, but anything that can be saved would be preferred.

Coleman Theatre Managing Director Report

Danny Dillon gave the Coleman Director’s report. The winter is the slower season, but the Coleman has lots of tours upcoming. There are shows upcoming including a comedy show January 22, The Whitney Houston Experience January 31, and an illusionist wanting to do 50 states in 50 days wants to come to the Coleman. A Selena tribute on February 20". Lots of shows upcoming.

Main Street Update:

Jesse Black gave an update on Main Street. The New Years Eve Ball Drop was held for the first time and was a huge success. PlacerAi registered 848 people and that only counts cell phones, so the numbers are estimated closer to 1000. Main Street held two workshops January 5 and 6. Marketing and Window Display. Those were requested by the Main Street Committees. There is a Sip and Shop coming up on February 10, and a new shopping event, Where’s Bless O’Parker the week of St. Patrick’s Day.
