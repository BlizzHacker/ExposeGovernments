About Miami, Oklahoma

Miami (pronounced my-AM-uh) is a city in Ottawa County, Oklahoma, United States. As the county seat of Ottawa County, Miami serves as the government and commercial hub for northeastern Oklahoma. The city is located near the borders of Kansas, Missouri, and Arkansas, making it a regional center for the Four State Area. Miami is home to approximately 12,000 residents and is governed by a city council-manager form of government.

Ottawa County is one of Oklahoma's 77 counties, with Miami as its county seat. The county encompasses several communities including Commerce, Quapaw, Wyandotte, Fairland, Afton, and Picher. Major highways include Interstate 44 (Will Rogers Turnpike), US-69, and US-59.
