⚖️ Courts & Legal Resources

🔍 Search Court Records

Search Oklahoma State Courts Network (OSCN) for Ottawa County cases — criminal, civil, family, probate, small claims. Results open in a new tab on OSCN.net.

💡 Tips: Search by last name + first name for best results. Case numbers use format like CF-2024-123 (criminal), CV-2024-456 (civil), or SC-2024-789 (small claims).

⚖️ Your Right to Know

Under the Oklahoma Open Records Act (51 O.S. § 24A.1 et seq.), every person has the right to inspect and copy public records from any government entity in Oklahoma. This includes emails, meeting minutes, financial records, contracts, and more.
