Why Texas works this way

Texas gives ten business days, but only if a request reaches an address the body has formally designated — and many designate none. That single technicality defeats most citizen requests before they start.

You do not have to wait for us

Texas's public records law covers every city, county, school district and state agency — not just the ones we have built a site for. The request we would file on your behalf is one you can file yourself today, and we will help you write it.

  1. Name a document, not a topic. “All agendas and minutes referencing X between these two dates” is hard to refuse. “Everything about X” invites a denial for being overly broad.
  2. Put the deadline in the letter. Citing the statute tells the custodian you know the clock is running.
  3. Ask for the exemption in writing. If they withhold, they must say which provision lets them.
  4. Escalate on paper. A refusal that exists in writing is the beginning of an appeal, not the end of a request.
