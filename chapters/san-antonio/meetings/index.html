Why you cannot search these agendas here

San Antonio publishes every meeting on its portal but serves the agenda document itself only to a signed-in account. We index what is public - the date, the body and the link - and cannot index the text. That is the City’s choice, not a gap in this archive, and it is the kind of thing worth asking about.
