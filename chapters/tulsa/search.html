Search tips

  • Two or three words beats a sentence. Every word must appear somewhere in the page.
  • Quote a phrase — "no quid pro quo" — to match it exactly.
  • Filter by category using the buttons above the results.
  • Agendas and minutes are indexed by their full text, so a phrase from inside a document will find the meeting that carried it. Those results open the city’s own copy — we index the words, they keep the file.
