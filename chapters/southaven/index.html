Southaven · DeSoto County · Mississippi

They promised hundreds of jobs.
The contract says twenty.

xAI's subsidiary MZX Tech, LLC is building a data center in Southaven under the codename “Project Synergy.” The City and DeSoto County signed an agreement abating two thirds of its property taxes for up to ten years. Everything on this site comes from documents the City itself released — not from rumour, and not from us.

Documented, promised, and still unanswered

A promise is not a contract, and a contract is not a receipt. This site keeps the three apart on every page. Community fact sheets circulating in July listed the size and length of the tax break as secrets nobody outside the room knew. They are not secret. They are written in the agreement the City released, and they are reproduced below.

✓ Documented

In a signed record you can read here
  • 67% of ad valorem taxes abated — the fee is a stated one third of taxes otherwise payable
  • Up to ten assessment years
  • At least twenty new jobs — the only job number in the contract
  • Binds future City and County boards, not just the ones that signed
  • $6,690,900 paid or pledged to the City and its police department
  • 16.5 million gallons of city water billed to MZX Tech in 132 days

? Promised

Said in public; no signed paper compels it
  • “Hundreds of permanent jobs” — no figure appears in the agreement
  • $60 million a year to DeSoto County by 2028
  • $20 million a year to Southaven's general fund
  • A $12.8 million sound wall
  • New police, fire apparatus and roads — contingent on money arriving

● Unanswered

What the released records do not show
  • What the abatement is worth in dollars. No official has published the total
  • Whether any board vote was ever taken in public on each payment
  • Whether air permits exist for the turbines at the site
  • What the schools receive under the apportionment
  • Any clawback if the twenty jobs never appear
How to close the gaps

Every item in the third column is a public records request away. Mississippi gives a custodian seven working days to answer, and if they refuse, the Mississippi Ethics Commission hears the complaint for free and can order the records released. We will file it for you, or you can file it yourself →

Southaven is the first, not the last

ExposeMississippi covers the whole state. Southaven is where it starts — because Southaven residents did the work first. The Safe and Sound Coalition filed the records requests that produced every document on this site, and DeSoto County is where the largest of these deals landed. That earns the first slot.

Each city gets its own subdomain as it comes online, and Southaven's home is permanently southaven.exposemississippi.com — so nothing published today breaks when the next town is added. Until then the front door lands here.

Another Mississippi town, same playbook?

Abatement negotiated behind closed doors, promises with no signed paper behind them, records requests that go unanswered — if that is happening where you live, tell us. The method on this site travels: the statute, the seven-working-day clock and the free Ethics Commission appeal are the same in all 82 counties.
