Before you dispute a water bill

The City bills a month behind, and a first bill on a new account can cover an unusual number of days — the Utilities Billing Manager confirmed both in writing. A single account can also have more than one meter. Ask for the read dates, the meter in and meter out, and the CCF used before you argue about the dollar amount.

Southaven bills in CCF (hundreds of cubic feet). One CCF is 748 gallons. How we read the City's bills →

Speaking at a meeting

What you say in a public meeting becomes part of the official minutes — a permanent record that auditors, reporters and courts can read later. An email to an alderman is not a record in the same way.

  1. Check the agenda first. It is posted before the meeting; find your item.
  2. Ask the City Clerk how to be recognised — 662-280-6554. Rules on sign-up and time limits are set locally and do change.
  3. Bring the document. Naming a specific record, date and dollar figure is far harder to deflect than a general complaint.
  4. Ask a question that needs an answer on the record. "Was there a recorded vote on the $1,380,900 donation, and on what date?" beats "I'm concerned about xAI."

Who publishes this page

The Move Weight Foundation — not the City of Southaven and not DeSoto County. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with no affiliation to either government, and nothing you submit to us reaches them unless you ask us to file it.

We publish this directory because a resident should not need to know which of three governments handles their problem before they can get it fixed, and because the same office that answers your pothole call is the office that holds the records nobody has published. Those are the same phone number. That is the point.

Every figure here is transcribed from an official published directory and dated. Governments reorganise and numbers change — if one is wrong, tell us and we will fix it and say when we did. For the City's own site, go to southaven.org; for the County, desotocountyms.gov.
