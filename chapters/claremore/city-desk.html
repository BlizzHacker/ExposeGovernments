Oklahoma Attorney General

Issues Open Records Act opinions. Oklahoma has no free citizen appeal forum — enforcement is a district-court action under § 24A.17.

Oklahoma State Auditor & Inspector

Audits every county and, on petition or request, municipalities. Published audits are public records.

Meetings, and how to be in the room

Claremore publishes its agendas through tribe_events: https://claremore.com/city-council/agendas-minutes/

Agendas are posted ahead of the meeting because the law requires it. That posting is the single most useful document in local government and almost nobody opens it — it is where a contract award, a rate change or a tax abatement appears in public for the first time, usually as one line in a consent agenda.

Reading a consent agenda

Items on a consent agenda are voted through together, with no discussion, in a few seconds. That is not a trick — it is how a body gets through routine business. But it means the only chance to see an item is before the meeting, in the packet. Any member can pull an item out for discussion, and so can a resident who asks one in time.
