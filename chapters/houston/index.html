Why Houston

Houston is the biggest public body in the state, and under the Public Information Act a body that wants to withhold must ask the Attorney General within ten business days and say why. Those requests and the rulings on them are themselves public. The pattern of what a city routinely asks permission to withhold is a map of what it does not want read.

The Move Weight Foundation's first chapter is a town of thirteen thousand people in Ottawa County, Oklahoma, and it now holds 522 archived meetings with machine transcripts. The second is San Angelo, Texas. The third is Southaven, Mississippi, where the City's own released documents show an ad valorem agreement abating two thirds of property tax on a recital of twenty new jobs. None of those started with a tip. They started with somebody reading the agenda.
