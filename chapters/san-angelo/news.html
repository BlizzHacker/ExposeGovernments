Who covers San Angelo

We are not a newsroom and we do not pretend to be one. We file records requests and publish documents. The reporting that made this project possible was done by four newsrooms, three of them operating on shrinking budgets. Read them. Subscribe to them.

Our policy

We link out. We do not copy, scrape, mirror, or republish other outlets' articles. Headlines below are pointers, not content — every one goes straight to the original publisher.

The outlets

SanAngeloLive

Independent, locally owned · sanangelolive.com

The most aggressive local coverage of the data center and the recall campaigns, plus a daily video newscast. Sections are unusually granular — separate beats for county, crime, politics, business, and San Angelo proper.

Useful sections: San Angelo · Politics · County · Business

ConchoValleyHomepage

Nexstar · KLST-TV (CBS) & KSAN-TV (NBC)

Runs a dedicated Concho Valley Data Centers beat and the closest reporting on council budget and tax rate votes.

2800 Armstrong St, San Angelo TX 76903 · Main 325-949-8800 · Newsroom 325-659-5324
News Director Cody Brown — cbrown@klst.net

San Angelo Standard-Times

Gannett / USA Today Network · gosanangelo.com

The city's daily paper of record. Most coverage sits behind a paywall; syndicated versions often surface on Yahoo News and AOL if you cannot subscribe.

34 W Harris Ave, San Angelo TX 76903

FOX West Texas

Tegna · KIDY (San Angelo) / KXVA (Abilene)

One newsroom covering both San Angelo and Abilene, so local depth is thinner — but broadcast coverage moves fast on breaking city stories.

Local news · Contact

KTXS

Sinclair · Abilene / regional

Covers San Angelo from Abilene. Filed useful reporting on the data center water hearings and the mayoral recall signature drive.

The Concho Observer

Independent / opinion · conchoobserver.com

Smaller independent outlet. Has published useful analytical pieces on the city's water position, including a widely-cited breakdown of the core water questions.
