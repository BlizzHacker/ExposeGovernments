Move Weight Foundation · San Angelo Chapter

San Angelo sold 343 acres of city land for a 1.2‑gigawatt data center.

No independent impact study was ever conducted — that is the City's own answer on its own website. Three ordinances governing the project passed in five weeks. Three council members have faced recall petitions over it. We are putting every document, vote, and number in one place, with a link to the source for each one.

Three things the record shows

Not opinions. Two are the City's own votes and words; the third is arithmetic on the City's own published figures, with the working shown so you can check it.

📅

126 days

Between the January 13, 2026 vote that made the data center permissible (6–1) and the May 19 adoption of the first ordinance governing it. The water ordinance came 154 days after. The binding decision preceded every rule written to constrain it.

See the dated votes →
📑

84.9%

Of the FY26 property tax increase falls on property already on the roll — $4,854,375 of $5,716,170, with only $861,795 coming from newly added property. The City published both halves; the ratio is ours.

See the arithmetic →
⚖️

47 signatures

Force a recall election in District 3 — because only 155 people voted there in May 2023. The same rule requires 2,971 to recall the at-large mayor. Taxpayers fund the election either way.

File a records request. We send it for you.

Texas gives you the right to almost every record San Angelo creates, under the Texas Public Information Act (Government Code ch. 552). Most people never use it, because the process is unfamiliar and because putting your name on a request to your own city government is uncomfortable.

Tell us what you want to see. The Move Weight Foundation files it as the requester, so your name never appears. We publish whatever comes back — including a refusal.

  • Free. Always.
  • Your identity is never disclosed to the city, county, or district.
  • The city has 10 business days to respond by law.
  • Every request and response is published publicly.

Records requests cost money. Copy fees, certified copies, postage.

San Angelo charges $0.10 a page, $2 to certify a report, $6 for an accident report, $7 for a clearance letter. A single budget-year document set can run into the hundreds of dollars. Every dollar donated goes to filing fees and the tools that publish what comes back.
