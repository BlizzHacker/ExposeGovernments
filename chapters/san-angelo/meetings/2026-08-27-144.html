Regular Agenda

a. DD26-12: 226 S Abe -- A request for downtown design approval to paint the exterior of an existing 
building, replace windows and doors, add concrete for parking in the rear and a lamp post, and add 
lantern style lights to the front located at 226 S Abe. 
Austin Reed, Planning & Development Administrator, presented the case. 
Board Member David shared that the building was built in 1959 and said it was considered historic. 
Board Member Donna stated that a building is considered historic if it is of a certain age, contains 
architectural significance, and is in high quality condition. She felt that the elements of the building consider . 
it historic. 

Aaron Vannoy said that this is not considered a historic building within the local ordinance. 
Board Member David wanted more clarification. 
Aaron Vannoy said that the state has guidelines of what a historic building is at the state level, but it is not 
considered as such at the local level. Aaron agreed that this is a high-quality Spanish style structure, but wants 
the decision to focus on what level of changes to the structure that the board would be comfortable with. 
Chair Chrys wanted to spe'nd more time looking at the design plans and photos, to refresh the perspective of 
the rest of the board, since this case was reviewed at the prior DHRC meeting. 
'1 
Board Member David thought the changes were too intense of chan·ges to the architecture. 
Board Member Brooks disagreed and stated that the changes would not affect the architectural quality or 
historic nature of the building. 
Board Member David had questions about the rear of the structure, Austin Reed requested those be deferred 
to the applicant. 
Chrys opened public comment. 
Jeff Padilla, the applicant, was available for questions. Jeff Padilla stated he had submitted side view 
renderings but they had not been present in the presentation. 
Board Member David asked if the building will have restrooms, Jeff Padilla said yes. 
Chair Chrys asked about the original roof tile colors, Jeff Padilla confirmed they were red and could go back 
to the original tile color if desired. 
• 
Aaron Vannoy said it appears that at some point the clay tiles were painted. Jeff Padilla said it would not be 
difficult to repaint the clay tiles. 
Aaron Vannoy asked about the new color of the additions, Jeff said it will be black. 
Board Member Brooks spoke to the architecture of the original design and wanted clarification on the 
building's function in respect to the openness. Jeff Padilla said it is an office space on the more enclosed side 
and it is very open on the garage door side. 
Board Member David wanted to ask what the building was planned to be, suggesting that if it was an event 
space that it would need handicapped spaces. Jeff Padilla said that the current focus is on the renovation of 
the building. 
Chair Chrys asked about not making the stucco changes, Jeff Padilla was open to that. 
Board Member David asked about paint colors, Austin Reed said it should have been in the staff report, Jeff 
Padilla said that the selected colors were off of the historic palette. 
Jeff Padilla recognized that Concho Ave is one of the entrances to downtown and this is an important building 
because of the traffic that it sees consistently. 
Chair Chrys likes the proposed work, but is concerned about modifying the stucco and harming the historic 
design aspects of it. 
Board Member Stacie likes the way it looks but is okay with not including stucco modifications. 
Board Member Stacie said that what is approved can be modified slightly and still be approved by staff. 

Board Member Donna applauds the interest that the applicant is taking in this building, and is still interested 
in seeing a different roof color and maintaining the look around the front door 
Chair Chrys closed public comment 
Board Member Brooks asked about buildings that are noncontributing non historic buildings and what design 
guidelines they need to follow 
Aaron Vannoy confirmed that they do not need to follow the historic guidelines and they just need to follow 
the downtown overlay standards. 
Board Member Brooks confirmed that a property owner has more de'velopment ability when it is not a historic 
building. 
Aaron Vannoy said that design decisions are made by the board and are always subject to appeal. 
Board Member David asked about if the building was instead on Chadbourne, or historic 
Aaron Vannoy said that it would then be held to a different set of standards. 
( 
Board Member Brooks confirmed that as part of a historic commission survey this building was not considered 
historic at any point in the past 
Board Member David confirmed that someone could request it become historic by a zone change to historic 
overlay. 
Chair Chrys asked about the stucco contributing to the historic nature 
Aaron Vannoy confirmed that this building is not considered historic, but if the board does not want to see 
changes to the stucco they are allowed to make that decision. 
Board Member David was concerned with the stucco modifications to the front of the building and the 
doorway 
Board Member Donna agreed that the changes to the front door don't maintain the look of the original 
building, and worries about painting of the clay tile roof and that the original clay color would be good. 
Chair Chrys reopened public comment. 
Jeff Padilla agreed with the changes that the board was interested in making to the roof and the front door. 
Board Member David wanted more clarification on the side of the building, Jeff Padilla said that they can 
keep it the same 
Vice Chair Luke said that would work. 
Board Member David made a motion to exclude changes to the side of the building and the front door and 
the clay tile be restored to the original terracotta. 
Jeff Padilla asked if terracotta is included in the historic palette 
Aaron Vannoy said that colors do not need to be in the historic palette. 
Chair Chrys closed public comment. 
Board Member Stacie seconded the motion. 
Motion passed 6-0 

b. CA26-08: 311 S Chadbourne - A request for approval of design to rebuild the parapet roof on the 
fac;:ade of the structure located at 311 S Chadbourne, with new material. 
Staff requested this agenda item be tabled to the next meeting of the Design & Historic Review Commission, 
as additional clarification was required from the applicant. 
Vice Chair Luke made a motion to table the item, Board Member Brooks seconded the motion, motion passed 
6-0. 
c. CA26-09: 1303 S Park St -- A request for approval of design to restore and renovate the historic 
property located at 1303 S Park St. 
Austin Reed, Planning & Development Administrator, presented the case. 
Aaron complimented the applicant and design professional for providing a high level of detail in the plans. 
Board Member David asked if they were extending the stone on the rear of the building, Aaron Vannoy said 
yes. 
Chair Chrys opened public comment. 
Debbie Evans, the homeowner, shared paint colors with the historic commission. 
Board Member David asked about the paint colors and what they were planned to be used for, Debbie Evans 
said the body will be white, the trim will be weathered shingle. 
Debbie Evans said that the proposed fencing will run along the south side of the property along the lot line. 
Board Member Brooks asked about the siding changes, Debbie Evans said they want to differentiate from the 
existing design while still maintaining the spirit of the original. 
Board Member David asked about the house being built on two lots, Debbie Evans confirmed it was on three 
lots. 
Debbie Evans confirmed that the house was built prior to 1910, and it has been in their family since it was 
built. 
Board Member Brooks asked about the proposed fencing. Debbie Evans said that new fencing would come 
along the front of the house where the driveway will be removed. 
Board Member David asked about driveway access. Debbie Evans said that the garage and access would be 
from the alleyway 
Board Member Brooks asked about if there was going to be fencing on the north side. Debbie Evans said that 
it will remain open on the north side but will be enclosed on the south side, for security. 
Al and Sandy Moreland, the contractors, were available for comment but did not speak. 
Chair Chrys closed public comment. 
Vice Chair Luke liked the precedent of renovation that this could set for the neighborhood. 
Chair Chrys reopened public comment. 
Debbie Evans said that the roof will be replaced with metal standing seam. 
Board Member David asked if removing shingles would violate the historic designation of it. 

Aaron Vannoy said that there were tin roofs back then, and that the shingles weren't considered a 
contributing factor. 
Debbie Evans said that the roof selection was colored to look old and weathered. 
Chair Chrys closed public comment. 
Board Member Stacie made a motion to approve, Board Member Brooks seconded the motion, motion passed 
6-0
