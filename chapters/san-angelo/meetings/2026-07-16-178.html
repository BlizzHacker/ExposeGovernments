Commission Member Donna was also unsure about proposed changes to the property

Chair Chrys asked about adding a condition for color of the roof as part of a motion, where the roof color

DESIGN AND HISTORIC REVIEW COMMISSION 
Staff Report –DD26-12: 226 S Abe 
July 16, 2026 
Additional Information: The applicant is formally seeking approval to repair and restore the existing 
building at 226 S Abe. The owner is looking to paint the building white, add concrete in the rear for 
parking and a lamp post, replace the windows and doors and add Hampton Bay Buxton lantern style 
lights to the front of the building.    
 
The building was established in 1959 and has been vacant for a few years. The applicant does not intend 
to change any structural components of the building.  
 
This applicant is planning to submit a TIRZ application.  
 
Recommendation:   
Staff recommend APPROVAL of case DD26-12, the request for downtown design approval to paint the 
exterior of an existing building, replace windows and doors, add concrete for parking in the rear and a 
lamp post, and add lantern style lights to the front for a property located at 226 S Abe, subject to two 
(2) conditions of approval: 
 
1. The colors, dimensions, and materials of all improvements shall be consistent with the 
renderings approved by the Design and Historic Review Commission.  
 
2. The applicant shall obtain all necessary permits from the Permits and Inspections Division.
