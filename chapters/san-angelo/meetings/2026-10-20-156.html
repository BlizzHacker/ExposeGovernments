Fort Concho Museum Board Minutes
August 18, 2026
Officer’s Quarters 8
|. Open Session
A. Call to Order — Board President Don Barnhart called the Meeting to Order at 3:33 PM.
B. Determination of a Quorum — Site Manager Bob Bluthardt confirmed a Quorum was present:
Don Barnhart, Monty Stanley, Lisa Marley, Larry Miller, Tracy Simpson, Ewell Loudermilk, Asher
Rogers, LtCol Johnathan Loveless, Dr. Michael Powers, Yukio Kuniyuki (arrived at 4:18), and
Tommy Earnest. Absent: Sarah Cole, George Balli, and Penny Roberts (in Court).
C. Approval of Excused Absences — Motion to excuse the Absences made by Ewell Loudermilk
and seconded by Larry Miller. Motion carried.
D. Introduction of Guests — COSA Parks Director Carl White
E. Presentations — No Presentations
F. Public Comment — No Public Comment
Lt Col Loveless introduced himself to the Board and told us about his service.
ll. Consent Agenda
4. Consideration of Approving the June 16, 2026 Minutes — the Minutes were Approved and
Accepted by Board consent.
2. Treasurer’s Report — Treasurer Lisa Marley presented the following report:
August 2026
June Income
e Overall, up about 5% over 2025 and second best of past 10 years.
e Admissions slightly down; Shop up 1/3 over last year.
e Special events income from art show fees.
July Income
e Down from 2025 but at or above last 10 years’ average.
e Heat and rain in mid-month affected admissions and shop.
e Rent lower but a late month deposit may have covered some of that gap for August.
YTD Income
e Overall, up 11% over this point in 2025.
e Admissions and Gift Shop both up.
e Membership awaiting August and September renewals and should meet projections.
e Rent at highest level of past decade.
YTD Expenses October-July 10 Months of 83.3% of Fiscal Year
e Overall, at 79.1% vs. 78.16 last year.
e Below the 83.3% YTD benchmark.
e Increases in operational expenses like utilities to be offset by personnel savings.
e Goal remains to balance.
Total Income Oct 25 — July 26 - $1,276,642
Total Expenses Oct 25 — July 26 - $1,238,246
BALANCE Positive $38,396
Motion to Accept the Treasurer's report made by Larry Miller and seconded by Ewell Loudermilk.
Motion carried.
3. Site Manager’s Report — Site Manager Bob Bluthardt directed the Board to his attached
report, highlighting Dr. Powers heading out the USA 250" speaker series, the APA Art show,
Cowboy Day on July 25, Chamber after Hours, Buffalo Soldier Day on July 28. Kudos to the
maintenance crew painting the Commissary interior. The exterior lighting project. Tourism Friendly
Community. Concho Cowboy company 20" anniversary. Several a/c units have taken a hit this
hot summer and required some tender loving care. Will be interviewing for a maintenance spot.
Bob noted the passing of Jim Kerbow, a long-time living history volunteer.

lll. Regular Agenda: Public Hearing and Comments
4. Fort Concho/USA 250" Updates — Dr. Powers reported on completion of the series he
headed out. Good turnout for the series. Per Bob, the September Speaker Series will continue
with the same theme. Brendalee Brown will be working on getting the speaker videos posted on
Facebook.

5. Review of Christmas at Old Fort Concho 2025 and Recommendations for 2026 Event —
Per Bob, Sarah Thieme working on Revolutionary War camp. May be cutting back on Media Day
for C@OFC. Had more people representing the event than media people covering the event. Will
carry this subject over to September.

6. Update on 2027 Chuck Wagon Cook-Off — Tommy reported on getting feedback from the
wagons that were here for the C@OFC demo event.

7. Final Report on 2026 San Angelo Gives Campaign and Project Schedule — Bob reported
that all the LED lights have been installed. FC has been successful in the past on funding tangible
items.

8. Review of Upcoming Events — Archeology Fair 9/19, Halloween events in Oct. Larry Miller
reported on the successful Fess Parker “Fess-tival’. Had 100 attending thru two sessions.

9. Collections report — Bob reported that FC Curator Cory Robinson is working on three
potential acquisitions; uniforms from the collection of Connie Lindmeyer via John Langellier,
military weapons from a local collector, and an 1870 carbine via John Langellier again. This
carbine was most likely assigned to a Buffalo soldier stationed at FC.

40. 2026 Membership Drive Report — Right now at $49K out of the $50K budgeted amount. Bob
passed out a membership list for the Board to peruse through.

11. Nominations Report — Nothing in the pipeline.

12. Review of FY 2026-2027 Budget Process — Two more City Council readings before
adoption.

43. Update on Fort Concho Social Media — Bob provided a printout of social media “clicks” on
data provided by McLaughlin Advertising. Bob with meet with ad agency next month to discuss
the Christmas event.

14. Fort Concho Foundation Report — Met today, investments doing good.

15. Update on Barracks 1-2 Visitor Center Project — Bob met with the architect. Texas
Historical Commission must bless any changes. Gift Shop moved to B1 from B2. Working on
funding.

16. Staff News and Reports — Rody Young new on the maintenance crew and Ray Robbins is
new museum guide.

17. Future Agenda Items & Considerations — Nothing noted from the Board.

Motion to Adjourn made by Larry Miller and Seconded by Tracy Simpson. Motion carried. Meeting
adjourned at 4:35 PM.

Next Board Meeting September 15, 2026.

Respectfully Submitted,

Tommy Earnest, Secretary
