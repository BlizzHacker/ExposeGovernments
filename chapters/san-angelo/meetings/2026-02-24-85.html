Civil Service Commission Agenda 
February 24, 2026 
 
Notice is hereby given of a regular meeting of the Civil Service Commission of City of San Angelo to be 
held on Tuesday, February 24, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. at the City Hall – East Mezzanine, 72 West College 
Avenue, San Angelo, Texas, for the purpose of considering the following agenda items:  
 
1. Call to Order 
2. Approval of the minutes of the previous meeting  
3. Approval of the daily action log 
4. Election and appointment of Chair and Vice Chair of Civil Service Commission 
5. Discussion and possible action regarding alleged electronic application submission issues affecting 
two applicants for the Fire Driver promotional examination administered on February 2, 2026. 
6. Discussion and possible action regarding amendment of the posted eligibility list for the Fire Driver 
promotional examination.  
7. Discussion and possible action of appeals from Fire Department Driver promotional examination 
administered on February 2, 2026 
a. Appeals of question 17 
b. Appeals of question 88 
8. Adjournment 
 
CERTIFICATION 
 
I hereby certify that the above notice of meeting was posted on the bulletin board at the City Hall of 
the City of San Angelo, Texas, on the 18th day of February 2026, at 5:00 p.m. 
 
 
 
 
 
All agenda items are subject to action. The Civil Service Commission reserves the right to consider business 
out of posted order and/or meet in a closed session on any agenda item should the need arise and if 
applicable pursuant to authorization by Title 5, Chapter 551, of the Texas Government Code. 
 
In compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the City of San Angelo will provide for reasonable 
accommodations for persons attending board or commission meetings. To better serve you, requests 
should be received 48 hours prior to the meetings. Please contact the ADA Coordinator, Angela Bloss, at 
325-657-4396. 
 
To provide our viewing public with information on the various City Boards and Commissions, this meeting 
will be recorded and available on the City YouTube page: www.youtube.com/cityofsanangelotx. For a 
schedule of all broadcasts, contact the Communications Department at 325-481-2727 or visit our website 
at www.sanangelo.gov.
Source: https://www.sanangelo.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_02242026-85
