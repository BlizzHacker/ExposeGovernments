Fairmount Cemetery Board Agenda 
Page 1 of 1 
February 10, 2026 
NOTICE OF A PUBLIC MEETING 
 
AN AGENDA OF A REGULAR MEETING OF THE FAIRMOUNT CEMETERY BOARD 
THE CITY OF SAN ANGELO, TEXAS 
9:30 A.M. – Wednesday, February 18, 2026 
Station 618 
618 S. Chadbourne  
 
THE FAIRMOUNT CEMETERY OFFICE IS ACCESSIBLE TO PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES.  
ACCESSIBLE ENTRIES AND SPECIALLY MARKED PARKING SPACES ARE AVAILABLE.  IF 
ADDITIONAL ASSISTANCE IS NEEDED TO OBSERVE OR COMMENT, PLEASE NOTIFY THE 
Fairmount Cemetery Office at 325 655-9475 AT LEAST 24 HOURS PRIOR TO THE MEETING. 
 
As a courtesy to those in attendance, please place your cell phone on “Silent” or “Vibrate” 
Thank You! 
 
I. 
OPEN SESSION  
A. Call to Order 
B. Public Comment   
The Board takes public comment on all items in the Regular Agenda.  Public input on a Regular Agenda 
item will be taken at its appropriate discussion.  Public input on an item not on the Agenda or Consent 
Agenda may be identified and requested for consideration by the Board at this time.  The Board may 
request an item to be placed on a future agenda, or for a Consent Agenda item, to be moved to the Regular 
Agenda for public comment. 
II. 
CONSENT AGENDA   
1. Consideration of approving the December 17, 2025 meeting minutes. 
III. REGULAR AGENDA: PUBLIC HEARING AND COMMENT  
1. Review of the monthly financial report for the months of December 2025 and January 2026. 
2. Discussion on fence project- Carl White. 
3. Discussion on exploration of future revenue opportunities-Beverly Stribling. 
4. Discussion of signage with Friends of Fairmount and Pleasant View-Susan Mertz. 
5. Report on the Friends of Fairmount activities and projects. 
6. Consideration of Future Agenda Items and Announcements. 
7. Adjournment. 
 
Given by order of the FAIRMOUNT CEMETERY BOARD and posted in accordance with Title 5, Texas 
Government Code, Chapter 551, on Tuesday, February 10, 2026. 
 
 
Jeremy S. Walker, Fairmount Cemetery Manager
