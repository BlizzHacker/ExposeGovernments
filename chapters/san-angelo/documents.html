Federal record — former police chief

Included because it is the documented precedent for why scrutiny of San Angelo's institutions is reasonable, not because it relates to the data center. Former Chief of Police Tim Vasquez was sentenced to 15½ years in federal prison and fined $35,000 for accepting bribes. The investigation was conducted by the FBI, the Tom Green County Sheriff's Office and the Texas Rangers, with the cooperation of the San Angelo Police Department and the City.
